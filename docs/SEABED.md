# The seabed, the wind, and what stays put

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-FR-SB-WHAT">Generated by `scripts/seabed.py`. Wind from the Open-Meteo ERA5 archive at a single grid point (1995-01-01 to 2025-12-31, [271,752](SOURCES.md#F-ba6227fdb8) hourly records), a table of fetch by direction and a table of onshore hours are read from files; the wave chain is computed, and its thresholds and the sulphide illustration are stated values.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-FR-SB-WHAT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## What is computed, and from what

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-FR-SB-MECH">The page follows the mechanism [D7 — Storm-driven resuspension](hypodrafts/D7.md "Storm-driven resuspension") names: waves stirring the bed in shallow water. It computes the orbital motion under waves from wind speed, direction, fetch and depth, and takes the shear it puts on the bed as what lifts sediment; currents are not computed here, and [CURRENTS.md](CURRENTS.md) takes them up.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-FR-SB-MECH "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-FR-SB-CHAIN">The chain: wind → fetch by direction, from the fetch table → fetch-limited wave height and period (Young & Verhagen 1996, shallow-water form) → bottom orbital velocity (linear wave theory) → bed shear stress (Swart friction factor) → compared against the critical stress for a living bed and a dead one.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-FR-SB-CHAIN "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## A live bed and a dead bed

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-FR-SB-TAU">The model's critical bed shear stress: **[0.1](SOURCES.md#F-6ff1ffc472) Pa** for a bed with no biology, **[0.5](SOURCES.md#F-51179a47ac) Pa** for one bound by biofilm, tube fauna and eelgrass - stated values in `scripts/waves.py`, whose comment attributes them to the cohesive-sediment literature without naming a document. Same weather, same waves: the only thing that differs between the beds is the threshold.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-FR-SB-TAU "What this claim rests on")</sup>

| Depth | Live bed | Dead bed | Ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| [3](SOURCES.md#F-8b8eb9b44c) m | [2,110](SOURCES.md#F-06f028317c) h/yr | [5,352](SOURCES.md#F-8ba7171857) h/yr | **[2.5](SOURCES.md#F-299170b575)×** |
| [5](SOURCES.md#F-5174a7b0b3) m | [683](SOURCES.md#F-ecb079351e) h/yr | [2,897](SOURCES.md#F-ff00e6d42f) h/yr | **[4.2](SOURCES.md#F-4efbb58744)×** |
| [10](SOURCES.md#F-000ba6561e) m | [89](SOURCES.md#F-2a00d8be2e) h/yr | [604](SOURCES.md#F-dae6ebc26b) h/yr | **[6.8](SOURCES.md#F-8395352c72)×** |

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-FR-SB-RATIO">At [10](SOURCES.md#F-000ba6561e) m the dead bed crosses its threshold [6.8](SOURCES.md#F-8395352c72) times as often as the live one, and the ratio grows with depth in the table.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-FR-SB-RATIO "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-FR-SB-LIGLAGEN">Prolonged oxygen depletion can leave white coatings of sulphur bacteria on the seabed, which DCE call liglagen: the bacteria use the last oxygen in the bottom water to oxidise hydrogen sulphide that has reached the top millimetres of the bed to elemental sulphur, and DCE call the mat the last barrier before the sulphide is released. Mat-forming sulphur bacteria such as *Beggiatoa* can also store nitrate and couple its reduction to the oxidation of reduced sulphur (Middelburg and Levin).</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-FR-SB-LIGLAGEN "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-FR-SB-SINK">Resuspending sulphidic sediment is itself an oxygen sink, with no nitrogen and no algae in it: reduced sulphur that rises into oxygenated water is oxidised there, most of the oxygen consumed in coastal sediments goes to such re-oxidation, and a storm can bring sulphidic water up into surface water (Middelburg and Levin). The register holds the mechanism as [E1 — Sulphide oxidation](HYPOTHESES.md "Sulphide oxidation").</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-FR-SB-SINK "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-FR-SB-ILLUS">At [50](SOURCES.md#F-b1107c1de7) µmol acid-volatile sulphide per gram of dry sediment and a dry bulk density of [0.5](SOURCES.md#F-f9f85ef6a2) g/cm³, stirring [5](SOURCES.md#F-00975e262e) cm would consume about [80](SOURCES.md#F-a22fc9cf48) g <span class="chem" data-chem="O2" title="oxygen (dissolved, as a molecule)">O₂</span>/m² if all the sulphide were oxidised to sulphate by the water's oxygen, against [80](SOURCES.md#F-10a113d93e) g <span class="chem" data-chem="O2" title="oxygen (dissolved, as a molecule)">O₂</span>/m² in a [10](SOURCES.md#F-5b8ef8c982) m water column holding [8](SOURCES.md#F-adfa2f3dc6) mg/L. These are values chosen for an illustration, with no source recorded, not measurements of Køge Bugt: the arithmetic says what they would imply, and nothing here says whether the bay's mud holds that much sulphide.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-FR-SB-ILLUS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

### The loop, as hypotheses

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-FR-SB-LOOP">The register holds the links of a loop as hypotheses, not findings: [D8 — Loss of biostabilisation, and the mobile bed](HYPOTHESES.md "Loss of biostabilisation, and the mobile bed"), [D7 — Storm-driven resuspension](hypodrafts/D7.md "Storm-driven resuspension"), [E1 — Sulphide oxidation](HYPOTHESES.md "Sulphide oxidation") and [F3 — Loss of eelgrass and macroalgae](hypodrafts/F3.md "Loss of eelgrass and macroalgae"). This page computes how often a bed with and without that binding crosses its threshold, and tests none of them.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-FR-SB-LOOP "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## What is not here

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-FR-SB-NOTCURRENTS">Currents. Whether wind-driven southward transport meets the Baltic outflow so that Køge Bugt collects material mobilised elsewhere is not established ([OPEN_PROBLEMS.md](OPEN_PROBLEMS.md)). Everything above is computed from wind, and says nothing about what arrives from elsewhere.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-FR-SB-NOTCURRENTS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-FR-SB-NOMEASURE">Also absent: any measurement. These are counts of hours in which the model's bed shear exceeds stated thresholds, not observations of sediment moving; the hours, and the ratio between the beds, depend on those thresholds and on the wave model, and nothing here checks either against an observation.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-FR-SB-NOMEASURE "What this claim rests on")</sup>
