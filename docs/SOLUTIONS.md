## 2. The plan does not separate it

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-PLAN-INTRO">Every catchment carries both a current sewer type and a planned one. Of the **[300](SOURCES.md#F-8ca57f52c7) combined catchments**:</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-PLAN-INTRO "What this claim rests on")</sup>

| Planned outcome | Catchments | What it means for the sea |
|---|---:|---|
| Stays combined | **[212](SOURCES.md#F-fbd3884100)** | nothing changes |
| *Separatkloakeret opland tilkoblet fællessystemet* | **[75](SOURCES.md#F-d918454fef)** | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-RECONNECT">separated inside the catchment - **and reconnected to the combined system.** The rain is still in the sewage pipe by the time it reaches an overflow.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-RECONNECT "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| Genuine separation, stormwater to a recipient | **[13](SOURCES.md#F-abf07e74ca)** | the rain leaves the sewage system |

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-MIDDLE">The middle row is the finding, and the category's own name says it. Separating the pipes within a catchment and joining them again upstream of the overflow changes what happens inside the block and nothing at the outfall.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-MIDDLE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-NET">By impervious area: combined drops from [3,447](SOURCES.md#F-8255ecde08) ha to [3,169](SOURCES.md#F-8985101e3b) ha, but [197](SOURCES.md#F-0be5e6504d) ha of that goes into the reconnected category. **Net area actually leaving the combined system: about [80](SOURCES.md#F-6587c0a1ae) ha, or [2.3](SOURCES.md#F-e8b111fd57)%.**</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-NET "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## 3. What has actually been disconnected

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-LAR-WHAT">The other route off the combined system is local infiltration - soakaways, rain gardens, green roofs - each of which takes a specific area of roof or pavement off the sewer. The city registers them individually, with the disconnected area in square metres.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-LAR-WHAT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

| | |
|---|---:|
| Registered installations | [1,444](SOURCES.md#F-a136f1d960) |
| Total disconnected area | **[156](SOURCES.md#F-7a29d6c544) ha** |
| Of which confirmed in use | [49](SOURCES.md#F-a44a30dbbc) ha |
| Combined-sewered impervious area | [3,447](SOURCES.md#F-8255ecde08) ha |
| **Share disconnected** | **[4.5](SOURCES.md#F-3bd21a291a)%** (confirmed in use: [1.4](SOURCES.md#F-d7d8e65001)%) |

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-LAR">So between [1.4](SOURCES.md#F-d7d8e65001)% (confirmed in use) and [4.5](SOURCES.md#F-3bd21a291a)% (registered) of the relevant area has been taken off the pipe. That is a statement about scale, not a criticism of infiltration as a technique.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-LAR "What this claim rests on")</sup>

### But a skybrudsvej is sized for the rain that does not cause overflows

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-DESIGN">The city's flood scenarios for the cloudburst plan are for a [100](SOURCES.md#F-abc8c14598)-year event. A skybrudsvej carries water only when the pipes are already overwhelmed; ordinary heavy rain still goes into the combined pipe.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-DESIGN "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-OFTEN">Overflows are counted in years, not centuries: in the national layer, the [2,541](SOURCES.md#F-8ef5570146) combined-sewer overflow structures that fill in the count report a median of [6](SOURCES.md#F-6ff754b92c) overflows a year.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-OFTEN "What this claim rests on")</sup> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-DIFFERENT">The surface network answers the flood risk to the city, which is a different problem with a different design storm.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-DIFFERENT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-EVERYDAY">The everyday-rain component is the green roads and the infiltration - [61](SOURCES.md#F-aef2de5e0a) km and [156](SOURCES.md#F-7a29d6c544) ha respectively - against [3,447](SOURCES.md#F-8255ecde08) ha that needs disconnecting.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-EVERYDAY "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-DELIVERY">Delivery, on the city's own expected-in-service dates: **[56](SOURCES.md#F-99886be938) km by the end of [2025](SOURCES.md#F-a05cfb5104)**, [148](SOURCES.md#F-ba02c2ffe5) km scheduled through [2038](SOURCES.md#F-1ab1337222), and [76](SOURCES.md#F-3686ef9ef0) km with no date at all.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-DELIVERY "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## 5. Why a representative sample cannot measure this

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-REGISTER">The national register holds **[19,665](SOURCES.md#F-ca585ebec0) rain-dependent discharge points** across [97](SOURCES.md#F-05fef0021b) municipalities ([680](SOURCES.md#F-1b6f67846e) of them inside the Copenhagen clip used elsewhere here). Only **[1,328](SOURCES.md#F-66b55fdb81)** of them carry a recorded basin volume; the other [18,337](SOURCES.md#F-9f877a5e0e) have the field empty. Among those that do have a number, the distribution is extreme:</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-REGISTER "What this claim rests on")</sup>

| Share of structures | Count | Share of stored volume |
|---|---:|---:|
| top [1](SOURCES.md#F-bccfed7608)% | [13](SOURCES.md#F-aee8a1ff8a) | **[24.0](SOURCES.md#F-c2c8fe6829)%** |
| top [5](SOURCES.md#F-23073d11f2)% | [66](SOURCES.md#F-87c76546cd) | **[50.5](SOURCES.md#F-590dca0120)%** |
| top [10](SOURCES.md#F-64b4f7a66c)% | [132](SOURCES.md#F-a4543f4d02) | **[64.1](SOURCES.md#F-b1f79aedcd)%** |
| top [20](SOURCES.md#F-bd946b7004)% | [265](SOURCES.md#F-5ac8a74857) | **[79.2](SOURCES.md#F-3c8c5cb5e7)%** |
| top [50](SOURCES.md#F-4dac00119b)% | [664](SOURCES.md#F-245830e8ff) | **[96.2](SOURCES.md#F-1a7b824fe9)%** |

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-TAIL">**[13](SOURCES.md#F-aee8a1ff8a) structures hold [24](SOURCES.md#F-c2c8fe6829)% of the recorded volume.** The largest is [42,000](SOURCES.md#F-88d0066948) m³; the median is [250](SOURCES.md#F-051c262d76) m³. That is a heavy tail.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-TAIL "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-TYPETAL">The national concentrations for hazardous substances in these discharges - the typetal - come from **[6](SOURCES.md#F-0019d1e160) combined-overflow stations and [5](SOURCES.md#F-c376e1041d) stormwater stations** in catchments chosen to represent households and residential areas. They cover only discharges without prior treatment such as settling in basins, and not industrial areas or heavily trafficked roads, which the report says typically carry more; and the report finds that a significant share of the adsorbing substances is caught in basin sediment.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-TYPETAL "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-TAILARG">Put those together. The stored volume is concentrated in a few structures; the concentrations describe the typical residential case and leave out the discharge types the report itself expects to carry more. A national load built as volume times typetal therefore rests on the typical case. Whether the load, like the volume, is concentrated in a few structures is not measured.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-TAILARG "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-PLASTIC">A similar concentration is documented for plastic in rivers: of [100,887](SOURCES.md#F-427fc66cc3) river outlets in a global model, [1656](SOURCES.md#F-14947ebe4a) account for [80](SOURCES.md#F-b05d8586b4)% of the plastic emitted to the ocean ([Meijer et al., *Science Advances*, 2021](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aaz5803)).</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-PLASTIC "What this claim rests on")</sup> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-ANALOGY">A sewer network need not be more evenly behaved than a river network, and this register's storage is not.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-ANALOGY "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## 6. How fast could any of this move?

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-TRACTABLE">The concentration in section 5 cuts both ways. It is what makes a typical-case estimate weak, and it is also what makes the problem tractable: if a few structures dominate, a few interventions cover most of it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-TRACTABLE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-TIMESCALES">The timescales below are this page's estimates, from what each step involves - equipment, an operating procedure, regulation, construction - not from any costed plan.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-TIMESCALES "What this claim rests on")</sup>

| | Timescale | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Measure the tail | **weeks** | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-MEASURE">Flow-proportional samplers on the largest [13](SOURCES.md#F-aee8a1ff8a) structures. That is videnniveau [5](SOURCES.md#F-3a70a9dc3f), the best method in Miljøstyrelsen's own hierarchy, at [30](SOURCES.md#F-00b2f8d525)% uncertainty instead of [135](SOURCES.md#F-16bd766a2e)% for a simple mass balance.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-MEASURE "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| Publish event-level data | **weeks** | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-PULS">PULS already holds the number of overflows a year. No flow rate is recorded at all, which is a decision about the format, not a limit of the instruments.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-PULS "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| Empty basins before the season | **months** | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-BASINS">Material caught in basin sediment can be carried out when a high flow scours it. Removing it in advance is an operating procedure, not construction.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-BASINS "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| Intercept fat at source | **months** | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-FAT">A grease separator sits at the business, so this is regulation and enforcement, not construction.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-FAT "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| Disconnect roofs at scale | **years to decades** | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-ROW-DISCONNECT">[1.4](SOURCES.md#F-d7d8e65001)-[4.5](SOURCES.md#F-3bd21a291a)% done.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-ROW-DISCONNECT "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| Genuinely separate the network | **decades** | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-ROW-SEPARATE">[13](SOURCES.md#F-abf07e74ca) of the [300](SOURCES.md#F-8ca57f52c7) combined catchments are planned for it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-ROW-SEPARATE "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| Deliver the surface network | **to [2038](SOURCES.md#F-1ab1337222)** | on the city's own dates |

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-SPLIT">So the answer to *how fast* splits in two. **The measurement problem could be addressed within a season**, and until it is, nobody can say which of the slow interventions is worth the money. **The infrastructure problem cannot be fixed quickly by anyone**, and the sewer plan on the books leaves most combined catchments combined.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-SPLIT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-S-FIRST">Which is the case for doing the cheap thing first. Instrument the tail, find out whether a handful of structures dominates the load the way a handful dominates the volume, and the argument about what to build next becomes an argument with evidence in it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-S-FIRST "What this claim rests on")</sup>
