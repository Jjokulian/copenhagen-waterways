<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-STATUS">**Each stage's status is this project's own reading of it against its gate, set out in its section, including where it is bad.**</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-STATUS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## 4. Testing hypotheses against the data — triage done

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-STAGE4">The triage is done, and drafts testing individual hypotheses against the data are written for [19](SOURCES.md#F-e4141721a3) of them.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-STAGE4 "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-THREE-THINGS">Each hypothesis needs three things before it can be tested, and most fail at one:</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-THREE-THINGS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

1. **An observable consequence.** Not "X contributes to Y" but "if X, then this
   measurable quantity behaves this way and not that way."
2. **A source that carries it.** Named, with its error class ([the taxonomy](https://github.com/Jjokulian/statistical-methods#7b-rate-a-data-stream-by-the-errors-it-can-contain)).
3. **A null under the constraint actually imposed** — not the statistic's nominal null.

The triage classes:

- **testable now** — consequence, source and null all available
- **blocked on a named fetch**
- **blocked on measurement resolution** — waiting on stage 3's remaining half
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-MICROBIAL">**unscoreable** — the deciding dimension has no column. *Microbial, viral and fungal community composition is the worked example: it decides [14](SOURCES.md#F-15dd43136e) hypotheses in the triage, [11](SOURCES.md#F-e661888b83) of them unscoreable, and national marine monitoring has never measured it.*</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-MICROBIAL "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-ABSENT">An unscoreable hypothesis is marked **unscoreable**, not scored zero. An absent dimension is not evidence of absence.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-ABSENT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-TIMEOFDAY">**Time of day belongs to a further class: the dimension is recorded, and the
sampling design seldom goes where it matters.** That is not an absent column and it is not a
testable question. It is a statement about when people are willing to be on a boat, and no
reanalysis of the archive can fix it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-TIMEOFDAY "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-CLOCK">ODA's water-chemistry extract carries a clock value on every genuine row of its
[1.8](SOURCES.md#F-539cbb5196) million — the [22](SOURCES.md#F-c20885b069) without one are fragments of a broken note field — and the
technical instruction asks for the sampling time in UTC ([the nutrients chapter, Kaas & Markager
1998](https://ecos.au.dk/fileadmin/ecos/Fagdatacentre/Marin/TA_NOVA_1998/Kap06.doc); *"Prøvetagningsdato og tidspunkt i UTC"*). A clock value is not always a time,
though: `scripts/clockzone.py` finds filled-in defaults on [27,934](SOURCES.md#F-2e4572ce1f) of [129,913](SOURCES.md#F-15d01e36eb) sampling visits,
and a convention that changes by era ([DATA_SOURCES.md](DATA_SOURCES.md)).</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-CLOCK "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-SAMPLING-HOURS">A clock hour is not a light level: at [56](SOURCES.md#F-17967350ac)°N, 09:00 on the winter
solstice is a sun [1.4](SOURCES.md#F-440fada5ac)° above the horizon and 09:00 on the summer solstice
[34.4](SOURCES.md#F-ee65ebe6c7)°. In the record since 2002 the 5th and 95th percentiles of observed
sampling times are [8.1](SOURCES.md#F-8dd62ef399) h and [15.6](SOURCES.md#F-49729dbadf) h local time; of [62,069](SOURCES.md#F-6d47856cfe) surface
oxygen samples with an observed time, [665](SOURCES.md#F-aaaa7cf960) were taken at night and [944](SOURCES.md#F-a90009b1e3) in
twilight. The pre-dawn minimum, which is the value an oxygen threshold is actually about, is
essentially never visited.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-SAMPLING-HOURS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-TRIAGE">**The triage** — [`hypodrafts/TRIAGE.md`](hypodrafts/TRIAGE.md) — classifies all [166](SOURCES.md#F-4196d405de) lettered hypotheses, one row each:</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-TRIAGE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

| class | n | share |

**Four things the shape says.**

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-VANDKEMI">**One extract was named as the blocker for nine.** ODA `vandkemi` (`Emne_10_11`)
is named in the blocker for [A1](hypodrafts/A1.md "Danish land-based nitrogen load"), [A2](HYPOTHESES.md "Phosphorus load"), [A5](HYPOTHESES.md "Advected nutrients from outside Denmark"), [A7](openproblems/A7.md "Sediment nutrient regeneration"), [B4](HYPOTHESES.md "Riverine particulate organic carbon"), [E2](HYPOTHESES.md "Nitrification demand"), [E11](HYPOTHESES.md "Ammonia toxicity"), [K1](hypodrafts/K1.md "Silicon depletion and the diatom-to-flagellate shift") and [K2](HYPOTHESES.md "Stoichiometric imbalance decides who grows").

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-ILTKOR">(`iltkor` is declared in `fetch_oda.py`'s topics and has never been fetched: the only one of its six topics with no file on disk.)</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-ILTKOR "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-GROUPS">**Not one hypothesis in group A is testable now.** All [10](SOURCES.md#F-b17d38223a) are blocked,

## 5. Mechanistic scoring — not started

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-MECH">"Mechanistic" is doing real work in that phrase. It does not mean *is there a
correlation*. It means: **does the mechanism's signature survive a change of measure-space?** A
mechanism is a relation that holds when other things change, so the score is invariance, not
fit.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-MECH "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-POSITIVE-CONTROL">A detector with demonstrated sensitivity that returns a null is informative; one without is not. Stage 5 needs such a positive control on this data before its nulls mean anything.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-POSITIVE-CONTROL "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-FIVE">**Each hypothesis gets, and none of the five is droppable:**</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-FIVE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

> a stability coefficient · at a stated level of organisation · against a stated null ·
> for a named functional · at a stated point in the accumulation of measure-spaces

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-VERDICT">Drop any one and it is a verdict, which is a different kind of object than this procedure can produce.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-VERDICT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-MEASURE-SPACES">**And it never terminates.** Each new independently produced measure-space is
another trial, so the score carries a time index and has no final value. Three production paths
today; and [74](SOURCES.md#F-24782dd4c7)% of CTD measurements carry a named probe — [81](SOURCES.md#F-9c2bc4c9bc) probe numbers in
all — so instruments are a further axis to compare across.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-MEASURE-SPACES "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## 6. Solution in its own spatiality — premature

The requirement, and it is the reason this stage cannot be borrowed:

> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-UNIT">**The unit follows the mechanism.** Not water bodies. Not municipalities. Not Denmark.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-UNIT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-BOUNDARIES6">Every one of those is a model boundary, failed or untested. The official water
bodies agree with the partitions the data produce no better than random connected regions of
the same sizes ([LANDING.md](LANDING.md)). "Denmark" as the unit for a marine account
presupposes a closed system across which nitrogen visibly moves — the same missing
denominator, at national scale.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-BOUNDARIES6 "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-GEOMETRY">So the geometry of the answer is whatever the surviving mechanism has: if it is
catchment-scale, the unit is the catchment; if a shoreline gradient, distance from shore; if
residence time, exchange time, and the map is not a map; if sediment memory, depositional
history, with depth as the axis.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-GEOMETRY "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-NO-TOTALS">**No national totals. No per-water-body allocations. No percentage of a residual.**</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-NO-TOTALS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-PROGRAMME">[PROGRAMME.md](PROGRAMME.md) proposes interventions ahead of stages four and five. It should be re-read against them when they exist, and the parts that assume an inherited geometry marked.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-PROGRAMME "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## 8. Try things — and why this is the last step, not the first

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-TRY">At the end of the sequence, and only at the end, something becomes legitimate that
would have been evasion at any earlier point: **stop trying to establish the cause, intervene,
and read the outcome.**</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-TRY "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-PLACES-LINK">[PLACES.md](PLACES.md) sets out what that would look like. This section is about *when you are entitled to it*.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-PLACES-LINK "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-ENTITLEMENT">**The entitlement is earned by the stages above, and every one of them is doing work in the trial.** Skip them and the trial is unreadable:</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-ENTITLEMENT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

| without stage | the trial fails because |
|---|---|
| **Stage 1** hypothesis landscape | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-WHY-S1">you cannot say what else it might have been, so a success attributes itself to whatever you already believed</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-WHY-S1 "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| **Stage 2** data sources | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-WHY-S2">you do not know whether the response variable exists — of the four public words for the damage, only one has a usable measurement behind it</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-WHY-S2 "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| **Stage 3** the view, held open | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-WHY-S3">**you have no *before*.** A trial without a baseline reads nothing, and the baseline is the expensive half</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-WHY-S3 "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| **Stage 4** the triage | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-WHY-S4">you do not know which mechanisms are unreachable, so you cannot tell "the trial failed" from "the trial could never have shown it"</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-WHY-S4 "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| **Stage 5** mechanistic scoring | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-WHY-S5">you have no null, so a change of the size weather produces reads as a result</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-WHY-S5 "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| **Stage 6** its own spatiality | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-WHY-S6">you run one trial nationally, and a response that differs from place to place averages away</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-WHY-S6 "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| **Stage 7** the social argument | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-WHY-S7">a result lands in a discourse that has no way to receive it, and is read as whichever side funded it</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-WHY-S7 "What this claim rests on")</sup> |

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-BOUNDARY">**So this is not the analysis failing.** It is the analysis returning a specific,
defensible answer: *here is the boundary of what observation can settle, and it is closer than
anyone assumed.* [42](SOURCES.md#F-198b20fb90) of [166](SOURCES.md#F-4196d405de) mechanisms are unscoreable and [20](SOURCES.md#F-64bb5d44db) more need an
experiment — **[37](SOURCES.md#F-2cc76f7f45)% of the field was never reachable this way**, and no amount of further
modelling moves it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-BOUNDARY "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-INTERVENE">Past that boundary, intervening is not a retreat from method. It is the only
remaining method, and it is a good one: setting a variable cuts every backdoor path by
construction, which an observational study on an archive whose sampling seldom visits the night
cannot do.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-INTERVENE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-ORDER-ARG">**The order is the argument.** "Just try things" said first is an excuse for not
knowing. Said last, after the field has been enumerated, the data characterised, the view held
open, the triage done and the spatiality established, it is the correct next move — and the
preceding work is what makes the result legible when it arrives.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-KP-ORDER-ARG "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## The rule that governs all eight

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-KP-RULE">Any output of this project takes the form of a coefficient with its qualifiers, or a
