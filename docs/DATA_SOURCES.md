## 9. Observed surface water  *(not in any source here; we collect it)*

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-DS-NO-OBS">None of the sources in this catalogue records where water is actually seen standing or running in Copenhagen's streets.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-DS-NO-OBS "What this claim rests on")</sup> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-DS-DP02-CALIB">Miljøstyrelsen's technical standard (dTA DP02) calibrates a model on measurement *at the overflow structure*: at least five clearly separable overflow events must be in the measured data.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-DS-DP02-CALIB "What this claim rests on")</sup> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-DS-NO-STREET">Calibration happens inside the pipes; nothing in the reporting chain observes a street surface.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-DS-NO-STREET "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-DS-CORNER">So a modelled depth at a street corner is never checked against the water actually standing there.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-DS-CORNER "What this claim rests on")</sup> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-DS-OBSLOG">`viz/log.html` (a phone-friendly, offline field logger) and
`scripts/observations.py` collect that layer. Records use the 2012 legend's depth classes up to
one metre, with everything deeper as one class, and flag drains that are *surcharging* —
water rising out of the gully, meaning the pipe below is full. They are stored in
`data/manual/observations.geojson`, hand-collected, never overwritten by a fetch script.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-DS-OBSLOG "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## 10. ODA — what the archive does not warn you about  *(fetched; read this before using it)*

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-DS-ODA-EXTRACTS">Four extracts are on disk: `kemi` (water chemistry, [1,805,827](SOURCES.md#F-9fa9c3c21c) rows),
`ctd` ([53,710,760](SOURCES.md#F-4df017f985)), `lys` (light attenuation, [2,370,091](SOURCES.md#F-60fa9bccaf)) and `maaledybde`
([151,203](SOURCES.md#F-34a9a3271a)), with dates from [1970](SOURCES.md#F-3609c5b182) to [2026](SOURCES.md#F-4c63726244).</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-DS-ODA-EXTRACTS "What this claim rests on")</sup> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-DS-ENUMS">`scripts/enums.py` counts every
distinct value of every column of `kemi` and `ctd`, and `scripts/ds_facts.py` counts the traps
below over every row of all four, because reading the top few values of a column is how a rare
sentinel survives.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-DS-ENUMS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-DS-UNITS">**Units are per row, not per parameter. [14](SOURCES.md#F-cb89b765bd) of [146](SOURCES.md#F-da414cb2c0) `kemi` parameters carry
more than one.** `Orthophosphat` is [565](SOURCES.md#F-d32d88f033) rows in µg/l against [353](SOURCES.md#F-c0e2d7eac1) in mg/l.
Integrated primary production splits [5,068](SOURCES.md#F-295cb26492) mg/(m²·d) against [62,831](SOURCES.md#F-afc6b57e29)
mg/(m³·d), *per area against per volume*, which cannot be reconciled without a depth.
`Klorofyl a` is [185,313](SOURCES.md#F-14ec5931d0) rows in µg/l and [1](SOURCES.md#F-00293e45ed) in mg/l — and no range filter can
catch that, because a plausible mg/l value is also a plausible µg/l value. **Check `Enhed` on
every row.** Oxygen, saturation and total nitrogen are single-valued throughout, which is luck rather than a guarantee.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-DS-UNITS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-DS-SENTINELS">**Sentinels, undeclared — and one announced but absent.** In `lys`, the
migration note on [1,857,147](SOURCES.md#F-a409ff4f46) rows says `YIntercept` was set to `9999999` because the
source lacked it; on [1,854,698](SOURCES.md#F-0bbe1b0168) of them the column is blank, and it holds
`9999999` on [0](SOURCES.md#F-6f7f2d24cf) rows of the extract. In `kemi`,
`GennemsnitsDybde_m` is exactly `99` on [4,332](SOURCES.md#F-3b39e7a914) rows, with nothing on the row to
say whether that depth was measured.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-DS-SENTINELS "What this claim rests on")</sup> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-DS-BOTTOM">In `maaledybde`, `BundDybde_m`
reaches [2300](SOURCES.md#F-39941b17c3) m at `Læsø rende`, where the station's [510](SOURCES.md#F-2d32cdf9c0) readings have a median of [22.565](SOURCES.md#F-e38eeb79ff) m,
and [292.3](SOURCES.md#F-708e892f0c) m at a station named `Hirtshals 15 m`, whose readings have a median of [17.5](SOURCES.md#F-fd4b1ba41e) m.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-DS-BOTTOM "What this claim rests on")</sup>
<span class="claim" data-claim="C-DS-LIGHT">`Lysprocent` exceeds [110](SOURCES.md#F-868ffe67c6)% of surface light on [21,348](SOURCES.md#F-62e5a3a339) rows and peaks at
[96057](SOURCES.md#F-2a9892712c). In `kemi`, oxygen saturation reaches [90971.8](SOURCES.md#F-b78fe39caf)%.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-DS-LIGHT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-DS-CENSOR">**Censoring hidden behind column names that do not say so.** `ResultatAttribut`
is `<` on [85,035](SOURCES.md#F-208361ba97) `kemi` rows, where `Resultat` holds *the detection limit, not the

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-DS-UTC">**What the instruction asks for and the archive does not deliver.** The nutrient

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-DS-KFAKTOR">**`KorrektionsFaktor` is not one thing.** In `ctd`, `KorrigeretResultat` equals

* <span class="claim" data-claim="C-DS-KF-OXYGEN">**Oxygen** ([Kap. 4](https://ecos.au.dk/fileadmin/ecos/Fagdatacentre/Marin/TA_NOVA_1998/Kap04.doc)): instrument bias compensation. The

  the ratio of the two. Where the Winkler bottle fell inside a gradient, the profile is corrected

* <span class="claim" data-claim="C-DS-KF-FLUOR">**Fluorescence** ([Kap. 2](https://ecos.au.dk/fileadmin/ecos/Fagdatacentre/Marin/TA_NOVA_1998/Kap02.doc)): **not** bias compensation. The

  **Do not filter fluorescence on this column.**</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-DS-KF-FLUOR "What this claim rests on")</sup>
