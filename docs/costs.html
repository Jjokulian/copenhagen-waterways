What the transformation costs

The companion to the sensing costing, for the other half of the argument: not what it costs to find out, but what it costs to do — taking Copenhagen's everyday rain out of the combined sewer, street by street, on the catchments that actually exist.

Every quantity on the left is measured — impervious area, person equivalents, street length, gullies and chambers come from the municipal sewer layer, the OSM road extract and the city's own structure register. Every price on the right is a stated range and a slider, because none of them is a quotation and the argument should survive somebody replacing them with real ones. The proposal being costed →
