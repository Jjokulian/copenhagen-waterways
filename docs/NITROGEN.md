<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-QC-CIRCULAR">The check tests conformity to the assumed concentration, not accuracy. ### The model's deciding input is itself a residual, and no field's balance is measured

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-SR353-INPUT">DCE's DK-QNP model computes the diffuse nitrogen load over the ungauged part of the country. Its own methods chapter names the key input:</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-SR353-INPUT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

> *"En vigtig modelvariabel i DK-QNP modellen til beregning af tilførsel af total diffus kvælstof er det årligt beregnede kvælstofoverskud på ’mark-niveau’."*
> — Thodsen et al. 2019, [DCE SR353](https://dce2.au.dk/pub/SR353.pdf), page 34

The **field surplus** (*markoverskud*). <span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-SR353-RELATION">And chapter 6 of the same report presents this as a result:</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-SR353-RELATION "What this claim rests on")</sup>

> *"Der er således – for perioden som helhed - en meget stærk, signifikant lineær relation mellem det nationale markoverskud og den samlede, normaliserede kvælstoftransport fra diffuse kilder (Figur [6.7](SOURCES.md#F-1dcb5d1282), D)."*

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-FIG67">**A variable that is an input to the model is correlated with the model's output, and the correlation is reported as a finding.** This does not make the attribution wrong. It makes `Figur 6.7 D` unable to evidence it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-FIG67 "What this claim rests on")</sup>

#### And that is a much narrower claim than it first looks

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-SR353-QUALIFY">**DCE qualify the figure themselves.** Right after presenting the relation, SR353 says that not all fjord catchments respond equally quickly — Mariager Fjord and parts of Limfjorden reduced their diffuse load *less* than *"relationen vist i Figur [6.7](SOURCES.md#F-83b8105bc4) D indikerer"*, citing two papers by Windolf and colleagues from 2012. They are pointing at the figure's limits, not resting on it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-SR353-QUALIFY "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-WINDOLF-UNREAD">A catchment-and-estuary study by Windolf, Blicher-Mathiesen, Carstensen & Kronvang, *Environmental Science and Policy*, 2012 ([10.1016/j.envsci.2012.08.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.envsci.2012.08.009)), addresses exactly how a fall in the surplus shows up in measured load. Its text could not be read here — the publisher's copy is paywalled and the one open copy listed for it has been withdrawn — so this page uses none of its findings.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-WINDOLF-UNREAD "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-BOGESTRAND">DCE also record that the DK-QNP method has a regional bias — overestimating nitrogen concentrations in western Denmark, tending to underestimate in the east — and correct the modelled monthly loads for it by region.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-BOGESTRAND "What this claim rests on")</sup> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-VALIDATION-OPEN">So the model has been evaluated. <span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-NARROW">**So the established claim is narrow:** *one figure, in one monitoring report, is structurally unable to support the inference it is used for.* The claim **not** established is that the attribution lacks evidence — and the independent support for it, which the studies above could supply, is not established here either.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-NARROW "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-SURPLUS-TERMS">And SR120 says what each term is made of: **mineral fertiliser** from national sales statistics, minus an estimated [5,000](SOURCES.md#F-3f004c665e) t N assumed to go to public grounds, forests and private gardens — a stated constant; **manure** from livestock **counts** by category, times a nutrient content per category that follows *"de til en hver tid gældende normer"* — the norms in force at the time, set by regulation; **yields** from the harvest census, with roughage known to be overestimated, so an assumed [10](SOURCES.md#F-630a3c20b6)% shrinkage for maize, grass and catch crops and [15](SOURCES.md#F-2399fde877)% for permanent grass; **crop nitrogen content** from the norm tables of the *Fodermiddeltabeller* of 1992, 1995, 2000 and 2005, with cereals *"korrigeres hvert år efter de analyser Videncenter for Svineproduktion foretager"* — corrected every year from analyses by the pig industry's own research centre; and **atmospheric deposition**, whose method is not read here.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-SURPLUS-TERMS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-NO-FIELD">**No field's nitrogen balance is observed.** Every term is a register count, a sales total or an area times a coefficient, and the surplus is what is left over; the one measurement inside it is the annual analysis of cereal nitrogen.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-NO-FIELD "What this claim rests on")</sup>

#### The consequence is documented in the sources themselves

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-NORM-STEPS">Because the coefficients are administrative, the number moves when they move, with no change in any field. In **1999** the nitrogen norm was cut [10](SOURCES.md#F-c889b9ac7d)%, dropping the quota by about [40,000](SOURCES.md#F-18304cc467) t N, while grass norms changed at the same time, raising it by about [15,000](SOURCES.md#F-01b6a90917) t N a year; SR120 calls the result a *"spring"* — a step — in the compiled series. In **2012** the method for nutrients removed at harvest changed from dry-matter yield to feed units, and SR120 records that its phosphorus net-input figures *"er steget lidt ift. de tidligere opgørelser"* — rose, from the method change alone. And **2013** is published **twice**, differing only in which register the fertiliser figure came from: **[84](SOURCES.md#F-8310e3e2ec) kg N/ha** from the *gødningsregnskaber*, **[81](SOURCES.md#F-b4ecc40fb3)** from Danmarks Statistik.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-NORM-STEPS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-REGISTER-CHOICE">That last one sets the scale. The surplus fell [148](SOURCES.md#F-c31639cde4) → [84](SOURCES.md#F-8310e3e2ec) kg N/ha over [22](SOURCES.md#F-eaafaf707c) years, [-2.9](SOURCES.md#F-3de7b503b8) kg/ha/yr. **Choosing which register to read is worth [1.0](SOURCES.md#F-bd0fd0b9d8) years of the trend** — small against the whole decline, not small against a year of it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-REGISTER-CHOICE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-OVERSEER">A coefficient model of farm nitrogen has been reviewed as a whole elsewhere: New Zealand's Overseer. The review panel, in its ministry's words, *"concluded they did not have confidence in"* Overseer's estimates of nitrogen lost from farms across the full range of the country's climates, topographies and land uses.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-OVERSEER "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-CHAIN">**So the chain reads:** norm tables × register counts → *markoverskud*, a residual → input to DK-QNP → diffuse load over the ungauged area → summed into total diffuse transport → correlated with *markoverskud* → "a very strong, significant linear relation". Each step is defensible alone. The composite cannot evidence what the last step is used to evidence.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-CHAIN "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-NEGATIVE">The monitoring register records, without a citation: In dry years such as 1996 and 2005 the calculated cultivation contribution comes out NEGATIVE - described in the literature as a meaningless result caused by the uncertainty in the assessment. <span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-DIFFUSE-BASIS">Underneath it, by the same register's uncited figures: **[240](SOURCES.md#F-44b234c71f) stream stations** covering **[49](SOURCES.md#F-d90ee38f36)%** of the country, the other **[51](SOURCES.md#F-e986da2740)%** modelled. ## 2b. Two subtractions, and the ~[70](SOURCES.md#F-a6e4df0589)% is not the one people think

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-TWO-SUBTRACTIONS">It is easy to run together the **field balance** and the **load apportionment**. They are different operations on different quantities, and the headline share is the second, not the first.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-TWO-SUBTRACTIONS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

| | **A — field surplus (*markoverskud*)** | **B — the ~[70](SOURCES.md#F-a6e4df0589)% share** |
|---|---|---|
| what it subtracts | harvest from inputs | point sources and background from river transport |
| units | kg N/ha | t N/yr, reported as a share |
| kind | **norm product** — counts × regulatory coefficients | **residual** — what is left after naming what could be named |
| fails when | a norm changes with no change in any field | an unnamed source exists |

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-COUPLED">They are coupled, which is why they get confused: **A is an input to DK-QNP, and DK-QNP supplies B's river-transport term over the modelled [51](SOURCES.md#F-e986da2740)% of the country.** So A partly determines the minuend of B. But the ~[70](SOURCES.md#F-a6e4df0589)% itself is B.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-COUPLED "What this claim rests on")</sup>

### B, from its own methods note

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-MSFD-DEF">The construction, in DCE's own words ([`MSFD notat 2-8`](https://dce.au.dk/fileadmin/dce.au.dk/Udgivelser/Havstrateginotater/2-8_MSFD_notat_tilfoersel_NPO.pdf)):</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-MSFD-DEF "What this claim rests on")</sup>

> *"Diffuse tilførsler (inkl. spredt bebyggelse) minus baggrundsbidraget (diffus antropogen)"*

with the subtracted terms defined there as:

- **Natural background** — *"et mål for den diffuse tilførsel der vil komme fra oplandet, hvis der ikke var menneskelig aktivitet og som bestemmes i mindre oplande med lav antropogen påvirkning"*.
- **Diffuse load** — *"belastning fra dyrkede arealer inkl. spredt bebyggelse, da tilførsler fra spredt bebyggelse ofte er svær at adskille fra øvrige diffuse kilder"*.

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-MSFD-FINDINGS">Three things follow, all from the document rather than from us. **The category is defined as unseparated**: scattered dwellings sit inside the agricultural term *because they could not be separated from it*, as the definition states. **The subtrahend is a transferred measurement**: background is not measured in the catchment it is subtracted from — small low-impact catchments stand in for the rest, a basket assumption at the centre of the arithmetic. And **the split is not annual**: *"For de enkelte år opdeles den diffuse belastning ikke i den naturlige baggrundsbelastning og den diffuse belastning ikke, da opdelingen specielt for fosfor og organisk stof er usikker, men det for [5](SOURCES.md#F-e2bef0278f) års gennemsnit er lavet en opdeling."* The background/anthropogenic division is made only on multi-year averages, because the split is too uncertain year by year, **so an annual agricultural share is not a thing this method produces**, and any year-on-year movement in a published share is an artefact of interpolating that split.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-MSFD-FINDINGS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

### Which is why biogas is a carbon technology and not a nitrogen one

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-BIOGAS">The obvious hope is that digestion deals with it. It does not. **Anaerobic digestion removes carbon, not nitrogen**: methane and <span class="chem" data-chem="CO2" title="carbon dioxide">CO₂</span> leave, and essentially all the nitrogen stays in the digestate — with *more* of it as ammonium than before, because digestion mineralises organic N. That is useful as fertiliser value and it is the opposite of removal. **The field is still the endpoint.**</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-BIOGAS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-EXITS">What a biogas plant does supply is the thing the manure never otherwise has: **a collection point**. The manure is already gathered, homogenised and pumped, which is where nitrogen can be taken out — and there are only three kinds of exit:</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-EXITS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-EXIT-CAPTURE">**Capture it as a product.** Separate the digestate into fibre and liquid, then strip ammonia from the liquid into an ammonium salt — a concentrated, transportable fertiliser that can leave the catchment or displace Haber-Bosch nitrogen. The nitrogen is not destroyed; it is made portable, which is what the field balance needs.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-EXIT-CAPTURE "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-EXIT-DESTROY">**Destroy it, which is literally a reduction.** Nitrify then **denitrify** the liquid fraction and the nitrogen leaves as <span class="chem" data-chem="N2" title="dinitrogen gas">N₂</span> — inert, and out of the reactive pool for good. This is the only true removal available, and it is the same reaction a constructed wetland performs slowly and for free. It costs energy, and a badly run plant emits <span class="chem" data-chem="N2O" title="nitrous oxide">N₂O</span> instead, which trades a water problem for a climate one.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-EXIT-DESTROY "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-EXIT-BURN">**Burn the fibre.** Destroys organic nitrogen and recovers phosphorus in ash, at the cost of flue-gas treatment. It is the P route more than the N route.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-EXIT-BURN "What this claim rests on")</sup>

### What the law does to the herd, if the arithmetic binds

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-L5">The instruments are in the public record rather than in this analysis. `L 5`, the fertilisation law, passed on 2026-09-03, [119](SOURCES.md#F-4d2c213b6e) medlemmer af Folketinget stemte for loven, mens [34](SOURCES.md#F-a987b8d1e3) stemte imod (TV 2) — what TV 2 called *"et massivt politisk flertal"*, a massive political majority. The ministry's December 2025 announcement gives each holding a quota for its discharges set by how much its catchment must cut, from 2027 (*"en kvote for deres udledninger ud fra"*), and calls the agreement a safety net under the Tripartite's land conversion (*"et sikkerhedsnet under den"*). Under the law, emission-based regulation replaces field regulation from 2027, and its sharper step, the kvælstofhammer, cannot strike before 2028 ([POLITICS.md](POLITICS.md)).</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-L5 "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-NOT-READ">**This project has not read the statute**, so what follows is inference from those instruments and from the balance above, and it is offered as a prediction that can be checked rather than as a reading of the law.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-NOT-READ "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-PREDICT-MINERAL">**A quota bites on mineral fertiliser first, because manure is not optional.** The bag is the free variable: a holding that must apply less nitrogen buys less of it. The slurry is already there, produced daily by animals that exist, and it has to be spread, stored, sold or processed. So the first years of a tightening quota look like a fertiliser reduction and leave the herd untouched.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-PREDICT-MINERAL "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-PREDICT-TRANSFER">**The constraint transfers when the quota falls below what the manure alone supplies.** At that point the arithmetic offers exactly four moves: fewer animals, more hectares to spread on, more nitrogen leaving in the crop, or **the manure nitrogen leaving the holding**. And this is where the design of the Tripartite matters more than its rhetoric: its main engine takes land *out* — wetland conversion, afforestation, set-aside — which raises manure nitrogen per remaining hectare with no change in any herd. **An area policy becomes a livestock policy without ever naming livestock.**</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-PREDICT-TRANSFER "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-DEMAND">So a byproduct that has to leave is the predictable consequence, and the market for it already half exists — inter-farm slurry contracts, separation plants, and the stripping route above, whose output is a concentrated ammonium salt that travels. **A quota is a demand curve for nitrogen capture**, in the way [PROGRAMME.md](PROGRAMME.md) argues the liability settlements are a demand curve for destruction — regulation creating an industry rather than only a cost.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-DEMAND "What this claim rests on")</sup>

Three things have to be said with it, because each of them can make the prediction wrong:

- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-CAVEAT-CATCHMENT">**The quota is per catchment, so the export has to cross a catchment line.** Moving slurry from one farm to its neighbour changes whose paperwork it is and not what the receiving water gets. Only nitrogen that leaves the catchment — or leaves the reactive pool altogether — is a reduction where it is being counted.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-CAVEAT-CATCHMENT "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-BRAK">**The pressure is capped by design.** The agreement fixes a *braklægningspunkt*, which *"fungerer som et fastsat loft for"* how high the regulatory pressure can go in each coastal catchment.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-BRAK "What this claim rests on")</sup> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-CAVEAT-BRAK">That is exactly the point at which the squeeze on land would start forcing the herd, so where it is set decides whether any of the above ever happens.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-CAVEAT-BRAK "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-CAVEAT-LOAD">**And the load may not follow the input.** How much of a surplus reaches the sea varies by catchment, and DCE's own qualification shows fjords that responded less than the national relation. A herd reduction is an input reduction, and an input reduction is not yet a load reduction — which is the same objection this project makes to the standard account, applied to a measure it would otherwise be tempted to endorse.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-CAVEAT-LOAD "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-BIOGAS-ANSWER">So the answer to *can biogas fix it* is: not by itself, and yes as infrastructure. **Digestion without a nitrogen step returns every kilogram to the same fields in a more available form.** With separation and stripping, or with denitrification, the same plant becomes the only place in the chain where the nitrogen can be made to go somewhere else — which is the honest version of the claim that the herd sets a floor: it sets one **unless the manure nitrogen is given an exit that is not a field.**</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-BIOGAS-ANSWER "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-ACCOUNTS-MASS">The nitrogen apportionment counts nitrogen mass, which bears on the first line only. The mechanistic fjord models used to set the targets do describe the sediment's sulphate reduction — *"sker oxidationen overvejende ved sulfatreduktion med deraf følgende produktion af sulfid"* — but the share that circulates is a share of nitrogen. The consequences:</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-ACCOUNTS-MASS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-AMMONIUM">**Ammonium carries its own demand.** An ammonium-rich discharge — which is what a basin that has gone anaerobic produces — consumes [4.57](SOURCES.md#F-6cd20104a1) g <span class="chem" data-chem="O2" title="oxygen (dissolved, as a molecule)">O₂</span> per g N on arrival, with no algae, no light and no delay. <span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-SULPHATE">**Sulphate makes the debt effectively unbounded.** Full-strength seawater holds ~[2700](SOURCES.md#F-4976143668) mg/l sulphate = [901](SOURCES.md#F-f277f8007e) mg/l as sulphur, against ~[8](SOURCES.md#F-691901e12c) mg/l dissolved oxygen. Fully reduced and later re-oxidised that is [1799](SOURCES.md#F-687deb5511) mg/l of demand — **[225](SOURCES.md#F-e409a73f2d)× the oxygen in the water**. Reducing just [0.44](SOURCES.md#F-0d114bd8f2)% of the sulphate pool stores a debt equal to all of it. Danish waters are brackish and hold proportionally less sulphate, which lowers the multiple and leaves the point. Once a basin goes anoxic the electron acceptor is effectively infinite, and every storm that mixes oxygen in spends it re-oxidising sulphide instead of restoring the water.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-SULPHATE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-SILICON">**And the ratio matters, not just the total.** Diatoms take up silicon and nitrogen at a molar ratio of about [1](SOURCES.md#F-54b63f588b). Fertiliser and sewage add N and P; neither adds silicon, which comes from rock weathering. Enrichment therefore raises N:Si, and when silicon runs out the community shifts from diatoms to flagellates and dinoflagellates. A composition change driven by a ratio, invisible to any nitrogen total.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-SILICON "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-POTENCY">There is no potency term anywhere in the accounting. A kilogram delivered in February into a mixed column counts identically to a kilogram delivered in July into a stratified fjord.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-POTENCY "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-TRANSPORT-READ">So the geometry is right about the plume and wrong about the event. **While the city is overflowing, the water runs north**: southward transport falls below baseline during overflow-scale rain and only clearly exceeds it [12](SOURCES.md#F-c6c72c26e2) hours later, by which time the plume is diluted and no longer traceable to an outfall.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-TRANSPORT-READ "What this claim rests on")</sup> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-FLUSH">And the current field flushes mid Køge Bugt over [20](SOURCES.md#F-d9d23e4db1) km in about [73](SOURCES.md#F-d44427aa79) days, against [3.6](SOURCES.md#F-7d832601e5) in the southern Sound, so what does arrive stays for months.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-FLUSH "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## What this does and does not establish

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-NOT-URBAN">**It does not establish that urban discharge is a large national source.** In the pathways register above, combined-sewer overflow is put at [0.2](SOURCES.md#F-fa54f5fa4f)–[4](SOURCES.md#F-a4bcbf857e) kt N/yr against [59](SOURCES.md#F-33a12f031b)–[71](SOURCES.md#F-2cc3054929) kt from Danish land by stream: a fraction even at its upper bound.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-NOT-URBAN "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-DIFFERENT-QUESTION">**It does establish that the published percentage answers a different question than the one it is used for.** It answers: *of the nitrogen we observe arriving by stream from Danish land, how much do we attribute to farming after subtracting our models of everything else?* It is read as: *how much of Denmark's marine nitrogen problem is agriculture?*</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-DIFFERENT-QUESTION "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-THREE-DIFFERENCES">Those differ by a closed set, a potency term, and a state variable. The accounts contain none of the three.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-NI-THREE-DIFFERENCES "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-NI-BOUNDS">**Several ranges in section 1 are constructed here, not published figures, and the register does not mark which** — so every range there should be read as this project's unless a source is given.
