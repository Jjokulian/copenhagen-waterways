The retrofit, dimensioned

The arrangement argued in the programme, drawn at its real sizes: a shallow rain line bored shaft to shaft, the existing sewer left where it is, and the two-storey shaft with the watertight pan that serves both. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.

This is an arrangement with stated dimensions, not a design. Thirteen of the fourteen numbers below are ordinary design conventions adopted so the geometry can exist — they are marked STATED and every one of them is adjustable. One is MEASURED from this project's own data. Nobody has surveyed a Copenhagen street for this, and the model does not pretend otherwise.
