# Experiments, not studies

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-I-TRIAGE">Of the [166](SOURCES.md#F-4196d405de) hypotheses this project triaged, [42](SOURCES.md#F-198b20fb90) are unscoreable because the deciding measurement is in none of the sources it surveyed, and [20](SOURCES.md#F-64bb5d44db) more need an experiment that no surveyed source reports.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-I-TRIAGE "What this claim rests on")</sup> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-I-WEAKER">That is untestable **with the monitoring and data that exist**, which is a different claim from untestable, and a weaker one. A national observing programme answers questions about what is happening; a manipulation with a control answers questions about what causes what, and several of the open questions here would yield to one that fits in a season and a small boat.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-I-WEAKER "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-I-FAIL">The distinction matters because a study and an experiment fail differently. An analysis of existing data can always be argued with — the confounders are real, the record is short, the aggregation lost the signal. An experiment with a control and a decision rule fixed in advance either falsifies the hypothesis or does not.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-I-FAIL "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-I-RULE">So every entry below states its decision rule **before** anyone runs it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-I-RULE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-I-STERILE">**The recurring design element is the sterilised control** — the same material, autoclaved or irradiated, run alongside the live one: the organisms taken out, the material left. It separates *the chemistry of this stuff* from *the organisms in it* in a single step, and that is exactly the distinction [T4](HYPOTHESES.md "Marine replant failure: negative sediment feedback") and [T5](HYPOTHESES.md "Loss of sediment suppressiveness") turn on.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-I-STERILE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## Three kinds of work, which are not interchangeable

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-K-EXPERIMENT">**Constructive and investigative — `experiment`** ([12](SOURCES.md#F-b06e0ffe4a) of [23](SOURCES.md#F-1a851cdb55) below). You change one thing and watch what follows. It *creates* the evidence, and it is the only kind that can establish causation, because the control is what rules out the alternatives. It costs money, time, access and usually permission.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-K-EXPERIMENT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-K-MEASUREMENT">**Investigative — `measurement`** ([5](SOURCES.md#F-6decb62c26) of [23](SOURCES.md#F-1a851cdb55) below). You observe something real that nobody recorded. It creates the *record* rather than the evidence: it can establish what is happening, where and when, but not why. It still requires being there.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-K-MEASUREMENT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-K-INSTRUMENT">**Build the means of measurement — `instrument`** ([3](SOURCES.md#F-711d520267) of [23](SOURCES.md#F-1a851cdb55) below). You make the thing that takes the reading, and put it where nobody was looking. It is a measurement project with a build phase, and it differs from the others in what it can be aimed at: **a network can be pointed at the assumptions of the existing monitoring**, not only at the sea. Whether one station can stand for a water body, whether monthly sampling sees a six-hour event, what an aggregation costs - all of those are questions about the instrument, and all of them are answerable by building a denser one beside it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-K-INSTRUMENT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-K-ANALYSIS">**Armchair — `analysis`** ([3](SOURCES.md#F-35d545af48) of [23](SOURCES.md#F-1a851cdb55) below). You work on what is already written down. It can establish consistency, bound magnitudes, expose contradictions and kill hypotheses - but it cannot establish causation, and it cannot recover a fact that was never recorded. **Everything this project has produced is of this kind**: none of the data it works on was measured by the project itself. A finding of the form *your evidence does not support what you claim* is a real result and a limited one.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-K-ANALYSIS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-K-NAMING">Naming them separately matters because they are not substitutes and they are not equally strong. Only an experiment establishes causation. Only a measurement can recover something nobody wrote down. Analysis is the cheapest and the weakest, and it is what a project like this one can do from a desk — so it should be honest that its output is of that kind, and that the step up in force comes from going and looking.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-K-NAMING "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## Small

### X1 — Does eelgrass fail because the sediment is sick?

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X1-FRAME">`experiment` · **Bears on:** [T4 — Marine replant failure: negative sediment feedback](HYPOTHESES.md "Marine replant failure: negative sediment feedback"), [T5 — Loss of sediment suppressiveness](HYPOTHESES.md "Loss of sediment suppressiveness"), [T2 — Loss of the sulphide-detoxifying symbiosis](HYPOTHESES.md "Loss of the sulphide-detoxifying symbiosis"), [T1 — Sulphide intrusion, gated by light](HYPOTHESES.md "Sulphide intrusion, gated by light")</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X1-FRAME "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X1-WHY">[T4](HYPOTHESES.md "Marine replant failure: negative sediment feedback") proposes that restoration plantings fail in sediment whose chemistry looks adequate - the marine form of what horticulture calls replant disease - and that the test is the one horticulture uses.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X1-WHY "What this claim rests on")</sup>

**Manipulate.** <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X1-MANIP">Transplant eelgrass into a site where restoration has failed, in six treatments: (a) as-is; (b) inoculum from a functioning bed in a **matched** setting - same salinity, sediment and thermal regime; (c) inoculum from a functioning but **mismatched** setting; (d) the matched inoculum autoclaved; (e) lucinid clams added; (f) matched inoculum plus clams.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X1-MANIP "What this claim rests on")</sup>

**Control.** <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X1-CTRL">Two contrasts carry it. **Live against sterilised** (b vs d) separates a chemical effect from a biological one, because sterilising takes the organisms out and leaves the material. **Matched against mismatched** (b vs c) tests local adaptation: whether a community does worse in a setting unlike the one it came from. If donor origin matters, the practical rule follows immediately. Plus untransplanted plots and a transplant into a functioning bed as the upper bound.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X1-CTRL "What this claim rests on")</sup>

**Measure.** <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X1-MEAS">Survival and shoot density at [3, 6 and 12](SOURCES.md#F-92f9a2323e) months; sulphide in root and rhizome tissue; porewater sulphide.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X1-MEAS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

**Decide, in advance.** <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X1-DECIDE">Live inoculum beats sterilised → the sediment's *biology* is the missing thing, and [T5](HYPOTHESES.md "Loss of sediment suppressiveness") holds: sediment inoculation is a restoration tool. Both inocula beat as-is equally → it is chemistry, not biology. Clams alone work → [T2](HYPOTHESES.md "Loss of the sulphide-detoxifying symbiosis"), and the intervention is fauna rather than sediment. Nothing works → sediment sickness is not why restoration fails here, and attention goes back to the water column.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X1-DECIDE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

*<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X1-NOTE">Among the sources this project surveyed, the nearest Danish trial is an eelgrass transplant in outer Horsens Fjord with bare-bottom and natural-bed controls. It followed the fauna that came back, not eelgrass survival against sediment condition, so this inoculation test was not found run in Denmark.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X1-NOTE "What this claim rests on")</sup>*

*<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X1-NOTE2">The human analogue is faecal microbiota transplant, which moves the microbes of a healthy donor into an unhealthy person, and stool banks now hold donor material for it. The marine version of the bank is `X18`.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X1-NOTE2 "What this claim rests on")</sup>*

### X18 — Bank the communities that still work, before they stop working

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X18-FRAME">`measurement` · **Bears on:** [T5 — Loss of sediment suppressiveness](HYPOTHESES.md "Loss of sediment suppressiveness"), [T11 — Occupancy is the function](HYPOTHESES.md "Occupancy is the function"), [W8 — Whoever founds the community keeps it](HYPOTHESES.md "Whoever founds the community keeps it"), [T4 — Marine replant failure: negative sediment feedback](HYPOTHESES.md "Marine replant failure: negative sediment feedback"), [L1 — The reference condition never existed](HYPOTHESES.md "The reference condition never existed")</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X18-FRAME "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X18-WHY">Restoring a community needs a source, and `X1` asks whether one from a matched setting works best. Medicine keeps such sources: stool banks hold donor material for faecal transplant. A marine bank of a site's own community cannot be made once that site has failed, and each site that fails is one donor fewer.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X18-WHY "What this claim rests on")</sup>

**Manipulate.** <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X18-MANIP">Nothing is manipulated. Collect and cryopreserve sediment and rhizosphere communities from the Danish sites that still function - eelgrass beds, mussel beds, undisturbed soft bottoms - with full physical metadata: salinity, sediment grain size, thermal regime, depth, exposure.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X18-MANIP "What this claim rests on")</sup>

**Control.** <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X18-CTRL">The metadata *is* the design. Without matched conditions recorded, a bank is a freezer full of mud whose donors cannot be paired to a recipient site, and `X1` is the test of whether pairing decides if an inoculum establishes.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X18-CTRL "What this claim rests on")</sup>

**Measure.** <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X18-MEAS">Community composition by sequencing at the time of collection, so that what was banked is documented rather than assumed, and so the archive doubles as a baseline for [T10](HYPOTHESES.md "Removing an organism whose role is unknown is not neutral") and [W8](HYPOTHESES.md "Whoever founds the community keeps it").</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X18-MEAS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

**Decide, in advance.** <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X18-DECIDE">There is no hypothesis to falsify here, which is why it is filed as measurement rather than experiment. What it produces is **optionality**: every later restoration attempt, and every test of whether donor origin matters, needs source material that either exists or does not. It also supplies a pre-disturbance baseline, which the source search for [T10](HYPOTHESES.md "Removing an organism whose role is unknown is not neutral") found for no Danish marine intervention.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X18-DECIDE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

*<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X18-NOTE">**It gets harder every year it is not done:** each site that degrades takes its community out of reach. A freezer, a coring tube, and somebody's time.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X18-NOTE "What this claim rests on")</sup>*

### X2 — Is eelgrass killed by darkness, or by sulphide that darkness lets in?

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X2-FRAME">`experiment` · **Bears on:** [T1 — Sulphide intrusion, gated by light](HYPOTHESES.md "Sulphide intrusion, gated by light"), [K11 — Light as a depleted resource](HYPOTHESES.md "Light as a depleted resource")</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X2-FRAME "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X2-WHY">[T1](HYPOTHESES.md "Sulphide intrusion, gated by light") proposes that eelgrass keeps sulphide out by leaking oxygen from its roots, powered by photosynthesis. If the leak is the mechanism, shading kills by poisoning rather than by starving.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X2-WHY "What this claim rests on")</sup>

**Manipulate.** <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X2-MANIP">Shade healthy eelgrass in situ with mesh screens at four light levels spanning the assumed requirement, for one growing season.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X2-MANIP "What this claim rests on")</sup>

**Control.** <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X2-CTRL">Unshaded plots, and open-mesh frames that shade nothing - so the frame's own hydrodynamic effect is separated from the shading.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X2-CTRL "What this claim rests on")</sup>

**Measure.** <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X2-MEAS">Tissue sulphide, shoot mortality, growth rate, and porewater sulphide, measured on the same schedule.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X2-MEAS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

**Decide, in advance.** <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X2-DECIDE">Tissue sulphide rises *before* growth falls → the proximate cause is sulphide intrusion, the light requirement is really a sulphide-defence requirement, and the number that matters is sediment sulphide rather than Kd. Growth falls with no sulphide rise → ordinary light limitation and the existing indicator is measuring the right thing.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X2-DECIDE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

*<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X2-NOTE">Distinguishes two mechanisms that make identical predictions for the indicator and completely different predictions for what to do about it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X2-NOTE "What this claim rests on")</sup>*

*<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X19-NOTE">The recording device is the phone observers already carry. The hard part is recruiting observers who will keep reporting nothing.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X19-NOTE "What this claim rests on")</sup>*

### X20 — Ask the people with the longest baseline, about dated events

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X20-FRAME">`measurement` · **Bears on:** [T1 — A living seabed lost](HYPOTHESES.md "A living seabed lost"), [T4 — Provisioning lost](HYPOTHESES.md "Provisioning lost"), [W2 — Settlement cue failure](HYPOTHESES.md "Settlement cue failure"), [D1 — Bottom trawling](hypodrafts/D1.md "Bottom trawling")</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X20-FRAME "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-X20-WHY">Fishermen who have hauled gear across the same ground for years have seen the bottom there, and none of the sources this project surveyed records what they saw: its fisheries sources are landings and swept area, not what the bottom looked and smelled like.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-X20-WHY "What this claim rests on")</sup>

### X9 — Does anything happen after an overflow?
