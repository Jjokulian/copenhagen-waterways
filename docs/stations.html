Measurements, where they were taken

Every marine measurement in this archive, at the position it was recorded, for the month it was recorded. Nothing is aggregated into a water body and nothing is interpolated between points. Empty sea is empty because nobody measured there, not because the value is low.

Layers are labelled by what kind of thing they are. Only one of them is measurement. The water-body view is here — it is the thing this page exists to be compared against.

Layers, by what they are

Cartography, not measurement — here so the dots can be placed by eye. It is the union of the national land catchments, filled without strokes so no administrative boundary is drawn. Not derived from the water body polygons, whose seaward edges are the boundary under audit.
Class 1–2: sensor value, quantised. Recoverable or boundable error. One dot = one station-month median.
Absence, drawn. Usually invisible on a map, and it is most of the map.
Class 7: model-as-datum. A drawn boundary, not an observation. Feature-subspace stability found it adds nothing beyond being a connected region of its size.
An archive with no impossible values is either clean or silently trimmed, and you cannot tell which from inside it. These are the proof it was not trimmed. Amber = beyond the chemistry but explicable; red = gross. Of 599 oxygen values above 15 mg/l, 517 sit between 15 and 20 with a median of 16.2 — bloom supersaturation, not error. An earlier version of this page called them all impossible and was wrong.

Only errors that land outside plausible bounds are ever visible; a decimal slip turning 0.86 into 8.6 is invisible forever. So the ~78 gross values are a lower bound on the gross-error rate and the only observable sample from that process. Trimming them destroys the sole estimator of the archive's own error rate while making it look cleaner — an operation that increases apparent confidence, which is class 7.

Two mechanisms readable in the tail: six bed values at exactly −0.1 mg/l give the sensor's zero offset at anoxia, free. Fourteen clustered tightly at −9.4 to −10.3 are not scattered noise but the signature of a missing-data code leaking through as a value. Neither survives a cleaning pass.

Inverse-distance weighting. It invents every pixel between the dots. Nothing here comes from the archive — the controls are yours and so is the result.
