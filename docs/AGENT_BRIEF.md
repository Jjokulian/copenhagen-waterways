# Standing brief

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-FOR">Given to every agent working on this project, and to anyone contributing.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-FOR "What this claim rests on")</sup>
<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-SHORT">It is short because it is meant to be obeyed rather than admired.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-SHORT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## 1. Nothing is given

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-ONE">Every count carries a rule for what counts as **one**, and that rule is invisible by the time the count is a number.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-ONE "What this claim rests on")</sup> Call it the **applefication
assumption**. <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-DECK">Point at a deck of cards and ask *how many?* — one deck, four suits, fifty-two cards, or however many atoms are in them. There is no answer until somebody has said what a one is.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-DECK "What this claim rests on")</sup>

So for any quantity you handle, three questions in order:

1. **What is the individuation?** What was treated as one thing, one kind, one member?
2. **Is it reasonable** for the use it is being put to?
3. **Is it stable?** — <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-STABLE">the only one with an empirical answer.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-STABLE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-HIDES">A number is where this hides best, because a number looks like the least theory-laden object in a dataset. Its theory happened upstream, at counting time, and nothing in the digit records it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-HIDES "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## 2. Say what kind of thing a quantity is

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-BROKEN">**This project has broken this rule itself**: a mean-square ratio was labelled an intraclass correlation and read with that statistic's zero.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-BROKEN "What this claim rests on")</sup>
<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-LABEL">When you name a quantity, label it. Never write "X is [69.6](SOURCES.md#F-0666c7bdae)%" when X is a residual over products of norm coefficients.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-LABEL "What this claim rests on")</sup>

| kind | what it means | example from this project |
|---|---|---|
| **measurement** | an instrument was in contact with the thing | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-EX-MEAS">near-bed oxygen from a sonde</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-EX-MEAS "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| **derived** | arithmetic on measurements | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-EX-DERIVED">salinity, computed from conductivity + temperature</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-EX-DERIVED "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| **estimator** | a statistic meant to approximate an estimand — **name both** | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-EX-EST">monthly median as a stand-in for monthly state</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-EX-EST "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| **residual** | total minus known parts; absorbs every error in the parts | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-EX-RESID">the [69.6](SOURCES.md#F-0666c7bdae)% agricultural attribution</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-EX-RESID "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| **construction** | a count under an individuation rule someone chose | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-EX-CONSTR">"[123](SOURCES.md#F-8b12d8de95) water bodies", "[1,415](SOURCES.md#F-5fb04be1c0) stations"</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-EX-CONSTR "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| **norm product** | a register count × a regulatory coefficient | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-EX-NORM">*markoverskud*, whose manure term is livestock counts × excretion norms</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-EX-NORM "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| **model output** | produced by a model, possibly calibrated to measurements | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-EX-MODEL">DK-QNP diffuse load over the ungauged area</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-EX-MODEL "What this claim rests on")</sup> |
| **threshold class** | a continuum cut at a number | <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-EX-THRESH">"hypoxia" as oxygen below [4](SOURCES.md#F-dad8fcad81) mg/l</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-EX-THRESH "What this claim rests on")</sup> |

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-COMPOUNDS">Two compounds are especially dangerous and both occur here:</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-COMPOUNDS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-COMP1">**A residual of norm products, used as a model input, then correlated with that model's output** and reported as a finding. The correlation is structural.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-COMP1 "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-COMP2">**A threshold class whose defining variable is deterministically coupled to another** — an oxygen concentration threshold is partly a thermometer, because solubility is a function of temperature and salinity.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-COMP2 "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## 3. Compute your null, never quote it

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-NULL">A statistic's published null is the null of the model it was derived under, **not of the constraint you actually imposed.**</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-NULL "What this claim rests on")</sup>
<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-NULLS">This project has quoted the wrong null itself. Two of the cases are recorded in the files its analyses write:</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-NULLS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-NULL-MSR">The mean-square ratio MSB/(MSB+MSW), reported as an intraclass correlation, was read as if chance were zero. Under random labels both mean squares estimate the same variance, so its chance value is [0.5](SOURCES.md#F-d66335678c).</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-NULL-MSR "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-NULL-ARI">The adjusted Rand index reads zero as chance, but between two random *connected* partitions of the stations with the official group sizes, chance is [0.368](SOURCES.md#F-f45237d773): contiguity alone makes partitions agree.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-NULL-ARI "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-DIRECTION">Both came out above the zero that was quoted. A null's value is not guessable from the statistic's name, which is the whole argument for computing it rather than quoting it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-DIRECTION "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## 4. The rest

- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-R-WB">**Water bodies are a model assumption, not a unit.** Do not aggregate into them. "Denmark" is not a closed system either.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-R-WB "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-R-ABSENT">**An absent dimension makes a hypothesis unscoreable, not refuted.** Do not let "no data" become "no effect".</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-R-ABSENT "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-R-FLAG">**Flag, never delete.** Outliers are the only observable sample from the error process. Trimming them destroys the sole estimator of the archive's own error rate while making it look cleaner.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-R-FLAG "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-R-DIST">**Say what a number is a distribution of.** A single figure asserts that everything it summarises was one kind of thing — and a distribution of the wrong thing is as uninterpretable as a mean.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-R-DIST "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-R-FOUND">**The data you have found is not all there is.** `fetch_oda.py` lists an ODA topic, `iltkor`, with no file on disk, and does not list the eelgrass, macroalgae and bottom-fauna topics at all. Independence between sources matters more than volume: a derived channel - salinity from conductivity and temperature, oxygen saturation from oxygen, temperature and salinity - is not an independent measurement.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-R-FOUND "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-R-FAB">**Do not fabricate.** "Not established" is a valid and expected answer. A source you cannot verify is a source you say you could not verify.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-R-FAB "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-R-STEEL">**Steelman before you report a failure.** A bad argument in a document does not mean there are no good ones elsewhere. Ask what the strongest version of the case would be, look for it, and say whether you looked.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-R-STEEL "What this claim rests on")</sup>
  <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-R-CIRC">This project published a circularity finding and had to narrow it within the hour: the authors qualified the figure themselves on the next page, and a paired-catchment study existed that nobody here had read.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-R-CIRC "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## 4b. Stated conventions

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-CAT">A category everyone uses and nobody defines is the applefication failure in its purest form.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-CAT "What this claim rests on")</sup>
<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-DRAFTS">Three drafts in this project split the stations into *summer-peaked* and *year-round* and worked with three different splits: **[293](SOURCES.md#F-118fb708b0)/[481](SOURCES.md#F-b587779ac1)**, a figure on file that one draft built on; **[282](SOURCES.md#F-1719766f06)/[480](SOURCES.md#F-afbaabffc3)**, on another draft's own rule; and **[426](SOURCES.md#F-73954f34be)/[427](SOURCES.md#F-6178580461)**, on a third's Rayleigh classification.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-DRAFTS "What this claim rests on")</sup>
<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-L3">The last is larger, taken together, than the [488](SOURCES.md#F-82b4d206bd) `oxy_bed` stations with at least [24](SOURCES.md#F-22b645cad0) observations, so it was reached with a lower minimum or none.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-L3 "What this claim rests on")</sup>

**The station seasonality convention, from here on:**

> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-CONV-Z">Classify on the Rayleigh statistic **Z = N·R²**, never on raw R.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-CONV-Z "What this claim rests on")</sup>
>
> - <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-CONV-SEAS">**seasonal** — Z > [3](SOURCES.md#F-b49622f395) (about p < [0.05](SOURCES.md#F-bae8d9078a)) *and* mean direction in June–September</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-CONV-SEAS "What this claim rests on")</sup>
> - <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-CONV-YEAR">**year-round** — Z ≤ [3](SOURCES.md#F-b49622f395) *and* N ≥ [24](SOURCES.md#F-22b645cad0), so the test had the power to reject</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-CONV-YEAR "What this claim rests on")</sup>
> - <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-CONV-NEITHER">**neither** — everything else. **A station with too few observations is not year-round; it is unclassified.**</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-CONV-NEITHER "What this claim rests on")</sup>
>
> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-BASIS">State the variable basis, since it changes the answer: on all nine variables, [885](SOURCES.md#F-eab06314ce) / [254](SOURCES.md#F-8afad8bacb) / [276](SOURCES.md#F-cc7ee21e0c). On `oxy_bed` alone, [357](SOURCES.md#F-b4c5951535) / [271](SOURCES.md#F-e96b1fd285) / [699](SOURCES.md#F-6d2948bae7).</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-BASIS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

**Why Z and not R.** <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-WHYR">R is [1](SOURCES.md#F-ae1c59a95e) whenever a station has a single observation — the direction is perfectly concentrated because there is only one of it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-WHYR "What this claim rests on")</sup>
<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-SCARCITY">`oxy_bed` has **[254](SOURCES.md#F-97f8a7c013) stations with exactly one observation**, and a raw-R rule with no minimum put all of them in the "strongly seasonal" group, where they were [39](SOURCES.md#F-55db667bac)% of it and [57](SOURCES.md#F-6ec777dd94)% had three observations or fewer.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-SCARCITY "What this claim rests on")</sup>
<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-FIX">The statistic was measuring scarcity. **Z = N·R² fixes this without an arbitrary cutoff**, because a single observation gives Z = [1](SOURCES.md#F-22ff675aa2) and fails on its own.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-FIX "What this claim rests on")</sup>

The general rule this is an instance of:

> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-RULE">**If a category decides a result, its definition is part of the result.** Write it down, state the parameter that moves it, and report what the answer is under the alternatives. A category used by three analyses under three unstated rules is not one category.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-RULE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## 5. What may be emitted

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-EMIT">A coefficient, at a stated level of organisation, against a stated null, for a named functional, at a stated point in the accumulation of measure-spaces. **Five qualifiers, none droppable.** Drop any and you have produced a verdict, which is a different kind of object than this procedure can make.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-EMIT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-NOTEMIT">Not emittable: *"X is real"*, *"X is not real"*, *"the result is in"*.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-NOTEMIT "What this claim rests on")</sup> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-EA-B-NOREFERENT">Those are not false — they have no referent, because the space of groupings and functionals is open.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-EA-B-NOREFERENT "What this claim rests on")</sup>
