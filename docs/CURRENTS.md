# Where the water goes

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-DATA">Generated by `scripts/currents.py`. Currents from the Open-Meteo Marine API, hourly, 2022-2026; wind and rain from the ERA5 archive, hourly, 1995-2025. Both free and keyless.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-DATA "What this claim rests on")</sup> Companion to [SEABED.md](SEABED.md), which computes what the wind lifts off the bed; this asks where it then goes.

## First, what this data cannot do

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-FLUSH">**In this field Køge Bugt takes [73](SOURCES.md#F-d44427aa79) days to move water [20](SOURCES.md#F-d9d23e4db1) km, and Aarhus Bugt takes [3.4](SOURCES.md#F-4f4a15420d)** - a factor of [22](SOURCES.md#F-2daf695c28). Køge Bugt has both the lowest mean speed in the set and the smallest residual: the water moves and ends up close to where it started.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-FLUSH "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-BELT">*One caveat.* A low residual can also mean strong reversing flow with little net - which is what the Great Belt's [23](SOURCES.md#F-73b3ca6a77) days is. The Belt has [2.7](SOURCES.md#F-e1df1fe74b)× Køge Bugt's mean speed: it moves water back and forth vigorously and averages out. Køge Bugt is slow *and* close to net-zero, and only the second of these is retention of the kind asked about here.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-BELT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

### What the oxygen survey sees

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-ILT-DEF">DCE call it iltsvind when the oxygen concentration is below [4](SOURCES.md#F-27c460c084) mg/l, and it develops at the bottom especially where the water column is stratified.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-ILT-DEF "What this claim rests on")</sup> <span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-ILT-OBS">In DCE's autumn surveys Aarhus Bugt was among the hardest-hit areas in October 2023 and still had sporadic moderate iltsvind late in November 2025; in both years DCE registered no iltsvind in Køge Bugt.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-ILT-OBS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-INVERT">So the survey and this table rank different properties. Iltsvind is a concentration at the bottom, likeliest where the water is layered; the table ranks whether the water goes anywhere, and on that Køge Bugt is the extreme case in this set. A water can keep what it receives without registering iltsvind, and a survey that counts iltsvind would not show it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-INVERT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-SYDFYN">**Det Sydfynske Øhav** comes out at [8.7](SOURCES.md#F-401df48142) days - retentive, but far from Køge Bugt - and DCE's surveys found iltsvind there in both years. On this evidence the two bays are not the same case. A field this coarse cannot resolve an archipelago, so this is weak evidence either way.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-SYDFYN "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## A negative result: the wind index does not work

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-INDEX-PLAN">The plan was to regress the strait flow on a wind-driven Baltic filling index and, if it held, use the [31](SOURCES.md#F-c876192c07)-year wind record to extend the current record. It does not hold.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-INDEX-PLAN "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-INDEX">Best correlation is **r = [0.44](SOURCES.md#F-cb7c5c3984) at a [24](SOURCES.md#F-d8b09a3b38) hour window**, with sign agreement of only **[61](SOURCES.md#F-3691f72688)%** over [34,968](SOURCES.md#F-1e23c922ab) hours. The correlation falls away at the longer windows - [336](SOURCES.md#F-5ba5419a6f) h gives [0.01](SOURCES.md#F-3168e26d73), [504](SOURCES.md#F-f91379728d) h gives [-0.05](SOURCES.md#F-706635ffa6), [720](SOURCES.md#F-358d6dca0b) h gives [-0.07](SOURCES.md#F-91cceacde8).</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-INDEX "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-INDEX-READ">A [24](SOURCES.md#F-d8b09a3b38)-hour optimum is a local wind response, not a basin filling and draining over weeks. Either the multi-week Baltic memory is not the dominant control on the Sound, or this coarse product cannot see it. **The extension over the long wind record is not available on this data**, and everything about currents here is as long as the current record.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-INDEX-READ "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## Does Copenhagen's overflow water go toward Køge Bugt?

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-TEST">Køge Bugt lies south of Copenhagen, and the residual current at the southern Sound point runs toward [43](SOURCES.md#F-36e59db979)°, away from it. So the claim can only hold in particular hours: the hours in which the city overflows would have to be hours in which the water runs south. The rain record and the current record overlap for [35,064](SOURCES.md#F-85c4cd73bb) hours, so this can be tested directly.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-TEST "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## What is still missing

- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-NEED-FIELD">**A current field that resolves the straits.** Copernicus Marine publishes a Baltic reanalysis at [1](SOURCES.md#F-d78d670a7b) nautical mile resolution. Everything here would be worth redoing on it, the retention result first.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-NEED-FIELD "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-NEED-TRACK">**Particle tracking.** Retention is a summary statistic. Where material released off Amager ends up needs trajectories, which need the field above.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-NEED-TRACK "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-NEED-LONGER">**A longer current record.** The wind index failed, so the current record cannot be extended backwards, and the event counts stay small.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-NEED-LONGER "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-CS-NEED-DENSITY">**Density.** The Sound lies between the Kattegat, at more than [30](SOURCES.md#F-7190c3b8ee) PSU, and the Baltic, at around [7](SOURCES.md#F-98f20349e1) PSU, and its surface stream is often northbound from the Baltic. One current per point cannot show whether a plume and the water beneath it are moving differently.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-CS-NEED-DENSITY "What this claim rests on")</sup>
