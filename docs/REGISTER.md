### Caveats

- klima_id <-> plan-number links are reconstructed by citation scanning, not official.
- m3 figures are classified as storage / annual_flow / unclear from their sentence; only 'storage' figures on pages citing exactly one klima_id are attributed to a structure.
- Depths and diameters come from planning prose, not survey. No open source gives surveyed invert levels for Copenhagen sewers.
