### Oxygen

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-ILT">The iltsvind programme adds **[92](SOURCES.md#F-8ad4a68679)–[107](SOURCES.md#F-8ad4a68679) more stations a year**, twice a month July–November (June–October in Limfjorden, where hypoxia starts earlier), monthly July–October in the open inner waters. <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-WINDOW">That window is set by mechanism — the stratified season is when stratification-driven hypoxia happens — so seasonal claims drawn from it are not circular. ## 3. Our critique, audited

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-RUTHLESS">Be ruthless. Finding our own errors is a success.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-RUTHLESS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

### False as first stated

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-F1">**`F1`. "There is no coefficient anywhere between nitrogen and any outcome."** False: DCE's Tabel 3 lists [79](SOURCES.md#F-897ba71ead) fitted indicator-models, and DHI's ecosystem model is validated against measurements, bottom-water oxygen among them.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-F1 "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-F2">**`F2`. "Nitrogen → oxygen depletion: no coefficient, no ventilation term, no state variable. A kilogram in February counts the same as a kilogram in July under a pycnocline."** False for the mechanistic models, which are 3D, run on meteorology, resolve stratification, carry oxygen at all depths year-round, include oxygen consumption, redox-dependent phosphate release, denitrification and ammonium efflux — and validate bottom-water oxygen at **R² [0.83](SOURCES.md#F-831b728fd3)**. Even the statistical models carry water-column stability and temperature as candidate variables, and select them: stability in [13](SOURCES.md#F-3c65ed3a7c) of the [79](SOURCES.md#F-897ba71ead) fitted models, water temperature in [13](SOURCES.md#F-13fdae5fd6).</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-F2 "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-F2-LEAD">**What survives is narrower, and it is stronger:**</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-F2-LEAD "What this claim rests on")</sup>

1. <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-F2-1">In the **statistical route**, the oxygen requirement is a binary trigger plus a judged flat [25](SOURCES.md#F-8be24edb3a)%, with no dose-response between hypoxia severity and required reduction. DCE writes *"det vurderes"*.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-F2-1 "What this claim rests on")</sup>
2. <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-F2-2">In the **mechanistic route**, oxygen is simulated and well validated and is *excluded from the indicator set* — only summer chlorophyll and summer Kd produce a requirement — because only intercalibrated quality elements were in scope.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-F2-2 "What this claim rests on")</sup>
3. <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-F2-3">In the **load accounting** that produces the [69.6](SOURCES.md#F-a6e4df0589)%, there is indeed no potency term of any kind.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-F2-3 "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-F2-EXCLUDED">The model that could compute this does compute it. The answer is excluded by the indicator set, not by the physics.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-F2-EXCLUDED "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-F3">**`F3`. "Atmospheric deposition ... is absent from every published apportionment."** Too broad. Deposition is an explicit input to the mechanistic models, and the Gothenburg reductions are applied in every reduction run. What holds is narrower: it is not a line in the source apportionment that produces the [69.6](SOURCES.md#F-a6e4df0589)%, which apportions the discharge to coastal waters by source.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-F3 "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-F4">**`F4`. "Sediment regeneration is not a source at all in the accounting frame."** False as a statement about the evidence base. DHI's sediment module carries C, N and P pools, mineralisation, iron-bound phosphate, denitrification and ammonium release, accumulating in autumn and winter and released over summer. What holds is narrower: sediment regeneration has no row in the *load accounting*, and what NOVANA measures is the pool, sampled once in the year when the sediment is most oxidised, January–February.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-F4 "What this claim rests on")</sup>

### Needs qualifying

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-Q1">**`Q1`. "Nobody has published the regression of iltsvind extent on load, wind work and bottom temperature."** A strong negative claim about the literature, not verified here. The second opinion's background analyses summarise Hansen & Rytter 2024 as saying that *"Iltsvindets udvikling i løbet af året reguleres væsentligst af bundvandstemperaturen og de aktuelle vejrmæssige forhold, men udbredt iltsvind forudsætter en forudgående stor tilførsel af næringsstoffer"* — a summary of exactly that analysis. The paper itself has not been read here, so what can be said is that we have not found the coefficients published.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-Q1 "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-Q2">**`Q2`. "Halve the load and the extremes do not move."** Three qualifications are missing. A comparison of chosen years cannot carry a trend in a series whose variance between years is large. The alternative to "no effect" is "worse without", which such a comparison cannot separate. And warmer water holds less oxygen, so a warming over the same period would work against the load reduction. Keep the observation and drop the word "test": the extremes are not obviously tracking the load, and a comparison of extremes cannot tell the two readings apart.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-Q2 "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-Q3">**`Q3`. "Most water bodies have one monitoring station."** True and now precise — but say *which parameter*. One station per water body is right for the hydrography and water chemistry that feed the statistical models. The eelgrass programme runs [502](SOURCES.md#F-e52eef3824) transects and the iltsvind programme adds [92](SOURCES.md#F-8ad4a68679)–[107](SOURCES.md#F-8ad4a68679) stations a year.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-Q3 "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-Q4">**`Q4`. "The models cannot represent state-dependence or alternative stable states."** False for the mechanistic models, true for the statistical models and the accounting. DHI model eelgrass as a state with feedbacks onto light and resuspension, and model *why* eelgrass fails to return — sandworm burial of seeds, drifting macroalgae damaging shoots, resuspension — noting that in some areas the model *"forudsiger, at ålegræsvegetationen ikke genetableres i et omfang som registreret i tidligere tider. Skal der ske en genetablering i disse områder, skal nogle af de beskrevne stressfaktorer begrænses."*</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-Q4 "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-Q4-OURS">That is our argument, in their document. The alternative-stable-states case is not a heterodox objection to Danish marine science: it is inside the Danish model, and it does not reach the policy instrument, which takes only summer chlorophyll and summer Kd.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-Q4-OURS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-Q5">**`Q5`. The residual-estimator claims about the [69.6](SOURCES.md#F-a6e4df0589)%** concern the load accounting, not the requirement chain, and are out of scope here; [NITROGEN.md](NITROGEN.md) takes them up.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-Q5 "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-Q6">**`Q6`. "Køge Bugt cannot qualify for iltsvind however bad it gets."** The mechanism is plausible, but "cannot, however bad it gets" is our inference, not DCE's. Their criterion is oxygen below [4](SOURCES.md#F-6f707414ef) mg/l in the bottom water, and their notes record no iltsvind in Køge Bugt in their 2023 and 2025 periods; whether the bay can meet the criterion at all is unresolved.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-Q6 "What this claim rests on")</sup>

### Survives

- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-S-FAUNA">**Soft-bottom fauna is sampled 1 March – 31 May.** Confirmed verbatim, and strengthened: [45](SOURCES.md#F-f56d43a81e)–[49](SOURCES.md#F-f56d43a81e) of [110](SOURCES.md#F-034ce16b03) stations in a given year.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-S-FAUNA "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-S-JUDGED">**The oxygen requirement is a judged figure, not a measured dose-response.** *"det vurderes."* It holds with the mechanistic-route qualification attached.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-S-JUDGED "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-S-KD">**Reaching the Kd target does not imply reaching the eelgrass target.** DCE says so.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-S-KD "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-S-FEDTE">**Fedtemøg has no national monitoring.** Confirmed by exhaustion of the NOVANA programme.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-S-FEDTE "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-S-CURRENCY">**Nitrogen mass is the wrong currency for an oxygen problem.** Unaffected.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-S-CURRENCY "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-S-SHAPE">**Køge Bugt is the wrong shape for the national instrument.** Strengthened — by their number, not ours: Danish land-based nitrogen explains [2.8](SOURCES.md#F-0be5ae4676)% of its chlorophyll indicator.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-S-SHAPE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## 4. Their conclusions and ours, side by side

- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-SB-COEF">**Is there a quantified nitrogen–outcome relationship?** Theirs: yes — [79](SOURCES.md#F-897ba71ead) fitted models, four ecosystem models, an [11](SOURCES.md#F-75c26281ab)-water-body ensemble. Ours, corrected: yes for summer chlorophyll and Kd; no for oxygen *as a policy quantity*; no for anything downstream of oxygen. Both are right, about different links: our error was saying "no coefficient" when we meant "not for the link the public argument turns on".</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-SB-COEF "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-SB-STRAT">**Stratification, temperature, seasonality in the models?** Theirs: yes, in three dimensions. Ours: concede completely. **Them.**</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-SB-STRAT "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-SB-SLOPE">**Is the slope causal?** Theirs: it is the modelled response to changing Danish N, all else equal, presented as such. Ours: a partial derivative with everything else frozen — *"alt andet ... holdt konstant"* — the right instrument for "what would reducing do", the wrong one for "what caused this". **Us**, and their documents agree in their own words: a claim about *use*, not about the science.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-SB-SLOPE "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-SB-UNC">**Is the uncertainty adequate?** Theirs: ±[9](SOURCES.md#F-303f29a1c1)% nationally, from a genuine two-method ensemble. Ours: method right, scope narrow, and they say so — [11](SOURCES.md#F-75c26281ab) of [119](SOURCES.md#F-a57e45676f) water bodies, slope only, measurement, status and target excluded, the national figure *"For illustrationens skyld"*. Us on scope, them on method; no dispute of fact.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-SB-UNC "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-SB-TARGET">**Are the chlorophyll targets right?** The model group: the VP3 values are the scientifically most correct. The taskforce, in line with the international panel: they diverge from the intercalibrated values and should be refitted. Ours: no position; the target is in the numerator. An internal Danish dispute, to be reported, not adjudicated.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-SB-TARGET "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-SB-AVG">**Is averaging five indicators right?** Theirs: a deliberate choice to avoid over-implementation. Ours: honest and defensible; the consequence is that no water body must reach all five targets, and [4](SOURCES.md#F-98a84817e4) of [7](SOURCES.md#F-c50f19554d) weight units come from banded or judged values. No factual dispute.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-SB-AVG "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-SB-KOEGE">**Is Køge Bugt in the frame?** Theirs: Danish land nitrogen explains [2.8](SOURCES.md#F-0be5ae4676)% of chlorophyll there. Ours: the same number. Agreement.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-SB-KOEGE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

## 5. Synthesis

### Common ground, stated honestly

- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-CG-CHL">Nitrogen loading does affect summer chlorophyll and water clarity: fitted in [22](SOURCES.md#F-5006fe516e) water bodies, simulated in [45](SOURCES.md#F-0f8f66c762).</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-CG-CHL "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-CG-GRADED">The effect is **strongly graded by geography** — [94](SOURCES.md#F-670782a76e)% of the chlorophyll indicator in Hjarbæk Fjord, [0.2](SOURCES.md#F-e6b4eb923f)% off Bornholm.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-CG-GRADED "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-CG-TRUTH">Neither side has ground truth. There is no documented "right" target loading, and DHI says so.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-CG-TRUTH "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-CG-HYPOXIA">Hypoxia is a joint product of organic matter supply, weather and hydrography. DCE says it, the models implement it, we argue it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-CG-HYPOXIA "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-CG-EELGRASS">Eelgrass loss is self-reinforcing and may not reverse when light returns. DHI models it and warns of it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-CG-EELGRASS "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-CG-SLOPE">The published uncertainty covers the slope only.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-CG-SLOPE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

### Where a reasonable person lands

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-LANDS">The Danish evidence base is **much better than a critic who has read only the summaries would guess, and much narrower than a defender who has read only the summaries would guess.**</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-LANDS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-SUPPORTS">It supports a well-founded statement of this form:</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-SUPPORTS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

> *Reducing Danish land-based nitrogen by X% would improve summer chlorophyll and water clarity in this water body by roughly Y%, assuming the climate does not change and our neighbours meet their commitments.*

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-NOTSUPPORT">It does not support *"nitrogen from agriculture causes N% of the oxygen depletion"*, and it says nothing about fedtemøg: none of the six pinned documents in the chain names it. The gap between what the science says and what the politics says is where this project belongs — and it is a defensible place to stand precisely because the scientists' own caveats are the evidence for it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-NOTSUPPORT "What this claim rests on")</sup>

### What would have to be true for them to be right and us wrong

- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-TR-STATS">Summer chlorophyll and summer Kd would have to be adequate sufficient statistics for ecological status, so that fixing them fixes the rest. The mechanistic models compute oxygen and were not asked to produce a requirement from it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-TR-STATS "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-TR-SLOPES">The fitted slopes would have to hold down to [30](SOURCES.md#F-c963946aa7)–[60](SOURCES.md#F-c963946aa7)% reductions. Both DCE and DHI say this is unverifiable and warn that uncertainty grows with distance from the calibration domain.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-TR-SLOPES "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-TR-REGIME">No regime shift. Both documents flag it and say it cannot be predicted.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-TR-REGIME "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-TR-BSAP">BSAP and the Gothenburg Protocol delivered by our neighbours, because every scenario assumes it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-TR-BSAP "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-TR-STATION">One station per water body spatially representative. DCE raises this and does not resolve it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-TR-STATION "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-TR-TARGETS">The VP3 chlorophyll targets right rather than the intercalibrated ones.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-TR-TARGETS "What this claim rests on")</sup>

### What would have to be true for us to be right and them wrong

- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-US-ROUTES">The unmeasured routes to shore biomass — direct organic delivery, killing in place, sediment regeneration — would have to be large relative to the growth route in the specific bays where damage is complained about. This project has no measurement of any of them.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-US-ROUTES "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-US-STATE">The state term would have to dominate the load term. Mechanistically supported; this project holds it as a hypothesis and has not quantified it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-US-STATE "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-US-EXTREMES">The extremes would have to be genuinely insensitive to load rather than merely noisy, and a comparison of extremes cannot tell the two apart.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-US-EXTREMES "What this claim rests on")</sup>
- <span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-US-FEDTE">Fedtemøg would have to have a different driver or season from what is assumed. Currently unfalsifiable in either direction, because no programme this project has found measures it.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-US-FEDTE "What this claim rests on")</sup>

### The honest asymmetry

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-ASYM">Their case rests on a documented method with stated limitations, applied beyond the range where those limitations were tested. Our case rests on identifying real omissions and has, so far, no measurement of its own for any of them.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-ASYM "What this claim rests on")</sup>

<span class="claim" data-claim="C-GC-G-ASYM2">**Those are not symmetrical positions.** The right conclusion is not that they are wrong. It is that the instrument is being asked a question it was not built for.</span><sup class="claim-mark">[†](CLAIMS.md#C-GC-G-ASYM2 "What this claim rests on")</sup>
