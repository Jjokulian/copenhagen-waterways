# D7 — Storm-driven resuspension

**Status: testable now — but not on the monthly panel.** `stations_series.json` carries
nine variables, none of them turbidity, at monthly median. A storm is 6–36 hours; the wave
model already here puts 61% of *all* hours at 3 m above the dead-bed threshold, so a monthly
median describes a mixture, not an event. **The monthly product cannot test D7.** The dated
raw record can, and it holds a transparency series nobody here has used.

## The observable consequence, stated so it can fail

Resuspension is depth-limited (orbital velocity falls as 1/sinh *kh*), substrate-limited
(only mobile fines suspend), and it *relaxes* — fine mud settles over days. Three predictions
per station-visit, net of station mean, month-of-year and year:

1. **Depth.** Clarity is degraded in the 0–5 days after an hour of critical-shear exceedance
   at that station, scaling with the depth attenuation term — large at ≤10 m,
   indistinguishable from zero at >20 m **in the same storms**.
2. **Substrate.** Present over GEUS *Mud and sandy mud* / *Muddy sand* / *Quaternary clay
   and silt*; absent over *Sand* / *Gravel and coarse sand* / *Till* / *Sedimentary rock*
   (layer counts verified in [`D1.md`](D1.md), not restated).
3. **Relaxation.** The coefficient decays monotonically over 0–10 days since exceedance; a
   step persisting for weeks is a season, not a storm.

**Falsified if** the within-station coefficient lies inside the null below; **or** the >20 m
control moves as much as the ≤10 m arm; **or** the coarse-substrate coefficient equals the
mud one; **or** the lag profile is flat. (1)–(3) separate resuspension from "winter is
murkier," which is also true and is not D7.

## The data, counted here, with error class

**Response — Secchi depth. `data/raw/oda/maaledybde.csv.gz` (1.9 MB gz), scanned in full:** 151,203 rows;
**96,709 station-dates carrying both `SigtDybde_m` and `BundDybde_m`**, at **949 stations**,
**1980-03-06 to 2026-09-07**. By bottom depth: ≤5 m 24,394 · 5–10 m 21,572 · 10–20 m 30,500 ·
>20 m 20,243. **943 stations join to a lon/lat** in `stations_series.json`, 498 of the 502
shallow ones; extent 8.09–14.90°E, 54.52–57.63°N. *Class 2:* read to 0.1 m, often 0.5 m.

- *Class 4 + informative censoring, on the treatment arm.* `SigtTilBund` is True for
  **9,998** rows — **9,815 at ≤10 m, so 21.4% of shallow readings are right-censored at the
  bed**, against 46 of 20,243 (0.23%) at >20 m. The disc hits bottom exactly when the water
  is clear, so censoring correlates with the predictor *and* differs between arms: an
  uncensored fit would manufacture prediction (1) from censoring alone. Model it as
  interval-censored ("≥ bottom depth"); the indicator is itself a coarse response.
- *Class 4.* `SigtDybdeMedVandkikkert` True **5,945**, False 75,199, **blank 15,549** — a
  viewing scope reads deeper, so one column holds two instruments and 16% do not say which.
  Clean shallow subset: **27,861**.
- *Class 4, parse hazard.* ~36 rows carry numbers where booleans belong (`1,1`, `458865`);
  `lys.csv.gz` has a latitude in a date field. Strict CSV parsing, not `split(";")`; add both
  to `scripts/formats.py`.
- *Class 6.* No clock time; Secchi depends on sun angle — PLAN.md's worked example applies.
  *Class 5:* the extract has `X_UTM32` twice and no `Y_UTM32`, so position comes from the
  station register.
- **Sampling is not random in the predictor.** Visits by month: Aug 12,446 vs Dec 4,618,
  2.7:1 — vessels do not sail in a gale. Hence a test on the relaxation tail rather than the
  storm hour: a design consequence, not a caveat.

**Second response, another instrument. `lys.csv.gz` (22.5 MB gz):**
`LyssvaekkelsesKoefficient` (K_d) — **2,370,091 valid rows, 62,848 station-dates, 566
stations, 1980–2026**, with depth and coordinates per measurement. A PAR photometer is
neither the eye nor the CTD sonde: **a separate production path**.

**Third response, same instrument as the oxygen.** `ctd.csv.gz` carries `Turbiditet` and
`FDOM` among its 17 `Parameter` values ([`openproblems/E7.md`](../openproblems/E7.md));
counts verified by full scan here: TURBCOUNTS. Sonde turbidity shares cast, vessel, date and
often sonde with the oxygen channel, so it is **not a separate production path** — another
channel on a sensor already counted once.

**Predictor, and its class.** Bed shear is **class 7 (model-as-datum)**, and this repo
already has one: `scripts/waves.py` → `data/derived/waves.json`, hourly 1995-01-01…2025-12-31
(271,752 records), ERA5 wind → 5° ray-cast fetch → Young & Verhagen 1996 → orbital velocity →
Swart friction. It has **never been confronted with an observation** and exists **for Køge
Bugt only**. Wind is the closer-to-observation end of that chain — ERA5 assimilates
anemometers — so the design drives τ from wind rather than from a wave field, and still
carries class 7 on τ.

## Procedure

Ray-cast fetch at 5° from the held OSM coastline for the 498 shallow stations and the >20 m
controls; run `waves.py`'s chain per station at its own `BundDybde_m` on the held hourly wind,
giving τ(station, hour). Per visit form E_ℓ = max τ in day ℓ before, ℓ = 0…10. Fit an
interval-censored (Tobit) Secchi model on E_ℓ — station, month-of-year and year absorbed —
interacted with depth and substrate class; repeat on K_d. Report per-lag coefficients, both
interactions, and the event count.

## The null, under the constraint actually imposed

The constraint: visits sit where and when they sit, storms are autocorrelated and hit every
station at once, and the seasonal cycles of storms and plankton overlap. A shuffle of τ
across visits violates all three and scores the seasonal cycle as signal.

- **Null 1, time.** Circular shift of the whole hourly τ series by a random offset of whole
  years ± up to 60 days, re-deriving the lags each draw. Preserves the seasonal cycle and the
  storm autocorrelation exactly; destroys only the registration between *this* storm and
  *this* visit.
- **Null 2, depth.** Keep τ and the visits intact; permute *bottom depth* among stations
  within substrate class and a 50 km neighbourhood. Kills prediction (1), preserves the rest
  — including that shallow stations are coastal.
- **Effective n is events, not visits.** One synoptic system covers the domain, so every
  station sampled in its wake observes one storm. Report distinct exceedance events and
  stations per event — never the 27,861.

## What it would license, and what it would not

Licensed: *a coefficient, at station-visit level, for the lagged transparency deficit per
unit exceedance, with a depth gradient and a substrate contrast, against those two nulls* —
and, if Secchi, K_d and later ocean-colour SPM agree, a stability coefficient across three
production paths. Not licensed: anything about oxygen. Transparency is not sulphide, and
D7's O1/O2 leg needs the resuspended material's chemistry (`fetch_oda.py sediment`). Nor
fedtemøg delivered to a shore — D7's other claim, needing stranding reports no source above
contains.
