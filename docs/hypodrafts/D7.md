# D7 — Storm-driven resuspension

**Status: testable now — but not on the monthly panel.** `stations_series.json` carries nine
variables, none of them turbidity, at monthly median. A storm is 6–36 hours; the wave model
already here puts 61% of *all* hours at 3 m above the dead-bed threshold, so a monthly median
describes a mixture, not an event. **The monthly product cannot test D7.** The dated raw
record can, and it holds transparency series nobody here has used.

## The observable consequence, stated so it can fail

Resuspension is depth-limited (orbital velocity falls as 1/sinh *kh*), substrate-limited
(only mobile fines suspend), and it *relaxes* — fine mud settles over days. Three predictions
per station-visit, net of station mean, month-of-year and year:

1. **Depth.** Clarity is degraded in the 0–5 days after an hour of critical-shear exceedance
   at that station, scaling with the depth attenuation term: large at ≤10 m, zero within
   noise at >20 m **in the same storms**.
2. **Substrate.** Present over GEUS *Mud and sandy mud* / *Muddy sand* / *Quaternary clay
   and silt*; absent over *Sand* / *Gravel and coarse sand* / *Till* / *Sedimentary rock*
   (layer counts verified in [`D1.md`](D1.md), not restated).
3. **Relaxation.** The coefficient decays monotonically over 0–10 days since exceedance; a
   step persisting for weeks is a season, not a storm.

**Falsified if** the within-station coefficient lies inside the null below; **or** the >20 m
control moves as much as the ≤10 m arm; **or** the coarse-substrate coefficient equals the mud
one; **or** the lag profile is flat. (1)–(3) separate D7 from "winter is murkier."

502 shallow; extent 8.09–14.90°E, 54.52–57.63°N. *Class 2:* read to 0.1 m, often 0.5 m.

  **9,998** rows — **9,815 at ≤10 m, so 21.4% of shallow readings are right-censored at the
  bed**, against 46 of 20,243 (0.23%) at >20 m. The disc hits bottom exactly when the water
  is clear, so censoring correlates with the predictor *and* differs between arms: an
  uncensored fit would manufacture prediction (1) from censoring alone. Model it

- *Class 6:* no clock time, and Secchi depends on sun angle — PLAN.md's worked example.

**Third response — turbidity is in the archive, and it is large.** Full scan of

**Predictor, and its class.** Bed shear is **class 7 (model-as-datum)**, and this repo
already has one: `scripts/waves.py` → `data/derived/waves.json`, hourly 1995-01-01…2025-12-31
(271,752 records), ERA5 wind → 5° ray-cast fetch → Young & Verhagen 1996 → orbital velocity →

Wind is the closer-to-observation end of the chain, so τ is driven from wind, not a wave
field.

## Procedure

Ray-cast fetch at 5° from the held OSM coastline for the 498 shallow stations and the >20 m
controls; run `waves.py`'s chain per station at its own `BundDybde_m`, giving τ(station, hour).
Per visit form E_ℓ = max τ in day ℓ before, ℓ = 0…10. Fit an interval-censored (Tobit) Secchi
model on E_ℓ — station, month-of-year, year absorbed — interacted with depth and substrate;
repeat on K_d and on 2022–2026 `Turbiditet`.

## The null, under the constraint actually imposed

The constraint: visits sit where and when they sit, storms are autocorrelated and hit every
station at once, and the storm and plankton seasons overlap. A shuffle of τ across visits
violates all three and scores the seasonal cycle as signal.

- **Null 1, time.** Circular-shift the whole hourly τ series by whole years ± up to 60 days,
  re-deriving the lags each draw. Preserves the seasonal cycle and storm autocorrelation
  exactly; destroys only the registration between *this* storm and *this* visit.
- **Null 2, depth.** Keep τ and the visits intact; permute *bottom depth* among stations
  within substrate class and a 50 km neighbourhood. Kills prediction (1), preserves the rest,
  including that shallow stations are coastal.
- **Effective n is events, not visits.** One synoptic system covers the domain, so every
  station sampled in its wake observes the same storm. Report distinct exceedance events and
  stations per event — never the 27,861.

## What it would license, and what it would not

Licensed: *a coefficient, at station-visit level, for the lagged transparency deficit per
unit exceedance, with a depth gradient and a substrate contrast, against those two nulls* —
and, if Secchi, K_d and later ocean-colour SPM agree, a stability coefficient across three
production paths. Not licensed: anything about oxygen — transparency is not sulphide, and
D7's O1/O2 leg needs the resuspended material's chemistry (`fetch_oda.py sediment`). Nor
fedtemøg reaching a shore, D7's other claim, which needs stranding reports no source above
carries.
