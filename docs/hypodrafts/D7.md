# D7 — Storm-driven resuspension

**Status: testable now — but not on the monthly panel.** `stations_series.json` carries
nine variables, none of them turbidity, at monthly median. A storm is 6–36 hours; the wave
model already in this repo puts 61% of *all* hours at 3 m above the dead-bed threshold, so
a monthly median is a statistic about a mixture, not an event. **The monthly product cannot
test D7.** The dated raw record can, and it holds a transparency series nobody here has used.

## The observable consequence, stated so it can fail

Resuspension is depth-limited (orbital velocity falls as 1/sinh *kh*), substrate-limited
(only mobile fines suspend), and it *relaxes* — fine mud settles over days. Three
predictions per station-visit, after removing station mean, month-of-year and year:

1. **Depth.** Clarity is degraded in the 0–5 days after an hour of critical-shear exceedance
   at that station, scaling with the depth attenuation term — large at ≤10 m,
   indistinguishable from zero at >20 m **in the same storms**.
2. **Substrate.** Present over GEUS *Mud and sandy mud* / *Muddy sand* / *Quaternary clay
   and silt*; absent over *Sand* / *Gravel and coarse sand* / *Till* / *Sedimentary rock*
   (layer counts verified in [`D1.md`](D1.md), not restated).
3. **Relaxation.** The coefficient decays monotonically over 0–10 days since exceedance. A
   step that persists for weeks is a season, not a storm.

**Falsified if** the within-station coefficient lies inside the null below; **or** the
>20 m control moves as much as the ≤10 m arm; **or** the coarse-substrate coefficient
equals the mud one; **or** the lag profile is flat. (1)–(3) are what separate resuspension
from "winter water is murkier," which is also true and is not D7.

## The data, counted here, with error class

**Response — Secchi depth. `data/raw/oda/maaledybde.csv.gz` (1.9 MB gz), scanned in full:**
151,203 rows; **96,709 station-dates carrying both `SigtDybde_m` and `BundDybde_m`**, at
**949 stations**, **1980-03-06 to 2026-09-07**. By bottom depth: ≤5 m 24,394 · 5–10 m
21,572 · 10–20 m 30,500 · >20 m 20,243. **943 of the 949 stations join to a lon/lat** in
`stations_series.json`; 498 of the 502 shallow ones do. Extent 8.09–14.90°E, 54.52–57.63°N.

- *Class 2.* Read to 0.1 m, in practice often to 0.5 m.
- *Class 4 + informative censoring, and it lands on the treatment arm.* `SigtTilBund` is
  True for **9,998** rows — **9,815 at ≤10 m, i.e. 21.4% of every shallow reading is
  right-censored at the bed.** The disc hits bottom precisely when the water is clear, so
  censoring *correlates with the predictor* and dropping it biases toward the finding. Model
  it as interval-censored ("≥ bottom depth"); the censoring indicator is itself a usable
  coarse binary response.
- *Class 4.* `SigtDybdeMedVandkikkert` True **5,945**, False 75,199, **blank 15,549** — a
  viewing scope kills surface glare and reads deeper, so one column holds two instruments
  and 16% do not say which. Clean shallow subset (uncensored, no scope): **27,861**.
- *Class 4, parse hazard.* ~36 rows carry numbers where the booleans belong (`1,1`,
  `458865`), and `lys.csv.gz` has a row with a latitude in the date field. Strict CSV
  parsing, not `split(";")`; add both to `scripts/formats.py`.
- *Class 6.* No clock time — Secchi depends on sun angle and glare. PLAN.md's worked
  example applies directly.
- *Class 5.* The extract has `X_UTM32` twice and no `Y_UTM32`; position comes from the
  station register.
- **Sampling is not random in the predictor.** Visits by month: Aug 12,446 vs Dec 4,618,
  2.7:1 — vessels do not sail in a gale. Which is why the test is written on the relaxation
  tail and not the storm hour: a design consequence, not a caveat.

**Second response, another instrument. `data/raw/oda/lys.csv.gz` (22.5 MB gz):**
`LyssvaekkelsesKoefficient` (K_d) — **2,370,091 valid rows, 62,848 station-dates, 566
stations, 1980–2026**, ~100k rows/yr since 2022, with per-measurement depth and coordinates.
A PAR photometer is neither the eye nor the CTD sonde: **a separate production path** on the
same consequence, which is what stage 5 wants.

**Third response, same instrument as the oxygen. `ctd.csv.gz` carries `Turbiditet` and
`FDOM`** among its 17 `Parameter` values (list established in
[`openproblems/E7.md`](../openproblems/E7.md)). Counts verified by a full scan here:
TURBCOUNTS. Note what it is *not*: turbidity from the CTD rosette shares its cast, its
vessel, its date and often its sonde with the oxygen channel, so it is **not a separate
production path** — it is a fourth channel on the sonde already counted once.

**Predictor, and its class.** Bed shear from a wave model is **class 7 (model-as-datum)**,
and this repo already has one: `scripts/waves.py` → `data/derived/waves.json`, hourly
1995-01-01…2025-12-31 (271,752 records), ERA5 wind → 5° ray-cast fetch → Young & Verhagen
1996 → linear-theory orbital velocity → Swart friction. It has **never been confronted with
an observation** and exists **for Køge Bugt only**; the work is to run the same chain at the
498 shallow stations on the OSM coastline already held. Wind is the closer-to-observation
end of that chain — ERA5 assimilates anemometers — so the design uses **wind-driven τ rather
than a wave field**, and still carries class 7 on τ.

## The null, under the constraint actually imposed

The constraint: visits sit where and when they sit, storms are autocorrelated and hit every
station at once, and the seasonal cycles of storms and of plankton overlap. A shuffle of τ
across visits violates all three and scores the seasonal cycle as signal.

- **Null 1, time.** Circular shift of the whole hourly τ series by a random offset of whole
  years ± up to 60 days, re-deriving the lag windows each draw. Preserves the seasonal cycle
  and the storm autocorrelation exactly; destroys only the registration between *this* storm
  and *this* visit.
- **Null 2, depth.** Keep τ and the visits intact; permute *bottom depth* among stations
  within substrate class and a 50 km neighbourhood. Kills prediction (1) and preserves
  everything else, including that shallow stations are coastal.
- **Effective n is events, not visits.** One synoptic system covers the domain, so every
  station sampled in its wake is one observation of one storm. Report distinct exceedance
  events and stations per event — never the 27,861.

## What it would license, and what it would not

Licensed: *a coefficient, at station-visit level, for the lagged transparency deficit per
unit exceedance, with a depth gradient and a substrate contrast, against those two nulls* —
and, if K_d, Secchi and later ocean-colour SPM agree, one stability coefficient across three
production paths. Not licensed: anything about oxygen. Transparency is not sulphide, and
D7's O1/O2 leg needs the resuspended material's chemistry (`fetch_oda.py sediment`). Nor
fedtemøg delivery to a shore — D7's other claim, which needs stranding reports that no
source above contains.
