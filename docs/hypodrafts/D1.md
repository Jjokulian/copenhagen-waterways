# D1 — Bottom trawling

**Status: testable.** HYPOTHESES.md calls VMS/AIS effort "the single most important
missing layer in this whole register". It is a 23.1 MB CC BY 4.0 zip. Nothing here has
been run and nothing downloaded; the fetch is step 1.

## The observable consequence, stated so it can fail

Trawling as an oxygen mechanism (not a fauna mechanism) says: gear dragging fine reduced
sediment resuspends sulphide and labile organics, which consume oxygen where released — at
the bed, within weeks, **and only where fine reduced sediments exist.** Per station-quarter:

1. **Within station.** Near-bed oxygen anomaly is lower in that station's own quarters
   following high subsurface swept-area ratio (SubsurfSAR) in its c-square than in its
   own quarters following low SubsurfSAR, after removing station mean, month-of-year and
   year.
2. **Substrate interaction.** The effect is present on *Mud and sandy mud* / *Muddy sand*
   / *Quaternary clay and silt* and absent on *Sand* / *Gravel and coarse sand* /
   *Till* / *Sedimentary rock*.
3. **Placebo.** No comparable effect in near-**surface** oxygen at the same stations.

**Falsified if:** the within-station coefficient lies inside the null distribution below;
**or** the coarse-substrate coefficient is indistinguishable from the mud one; **or** the
surface placebo moves with effort as strongly as the bed. (2) and (3) are what make this a
test of trawling rather than of where fish are.

## The data, named, counted, and classed

**In repo.** `docs/data/areas/stations_series.{json,bin}` — 679,422 station-months, 1,415
stations, 1980–2026, monthly median per station per variable. Verified by reading the
binary: `oxy_bed` 76,980 station-months total, **8,396 in 2016–2021 at 224 stations**, of
which 197 stations cover ≥8 distinct quarters, 180 ≥12, 140 ≥16, 79 ≥20. Stations occupy
**791 distinct 0.05° cells**; 215 carry `oxy_bed` in-window, 175 in ≥12 quarters. `fluo`
7,694 in-window station-months, **7,655 of them sharing a station-month with `oxy_bed`** —
the productivity covariate is nearly complete on the response. `oxy_surf` 76,963 total
(placebo). 1,392 of 1,415 stations lie east of 8°E. *Class 3 (aggregation):* monthly medians
over irregular casts, so a station-quarter is a median of medians. *Class 2* for `fluo`, a
relative proxy, not chlorophyll.

**To fetch (small).** ICES/HELCOM VMS product, figshare 20310255,

**Substrate.** `data/raw/geus/seabed_sediment_dk.gpkg`, layer `Seabed_sedimen`, EPSG:25832

## The null, under the constraint actually imposed

Two arms, two constraints, two nulls, **neither a plain shuffle.** Effort is spatially
autocorrelated and so is bathymetry; this project has measured what that alone buys — two
contiguity-constrained partitions of these stations agree at ARI 0.337 against a nominal 0
(`docs/data/areas/partition_contiguous.json`, `contiguous_null` in
`scripts/partition_contiguous.py`). A cell-wise shuffle repeats that mistake.

- **Spatial arm** (cross-sectional, the weak one). Constraint: stations sit where they sit
  and effort has the structure it has. Null: keep the effort field whole, break only its
  *registration* with the stations — random rotations and toroidal translations within a
  Danish sea mask, rejecting draws putting >5% of stations on no-data; plus a second null by
  `contiguous_null`-style connected block resampling on the c-square adjacency graph with
  matched block sizes. ≥999 draws, reported against both.
- **Within-station arm** (primary). Constraint: station identity, month-of-year and year
  are absorbed. Null: circular shifts of each station's own effort series by whole years,
  plus a cluster bootstrap over stations. This preserves each station's effort marginal, the
  seasonal cycle and effort's serial correlation, breaking only the time alignment.

## Procedure

1. Fetch and unzip the 23 MB product; keep `geargroup` in the mobile bottom-contacting
   set (otter trawl, beam trawl, dredge); count c-squares intersecting the station bounding
   box — **not established here.**
2. Assign each cast to its own c-square from its measurement coordinates; roll to
   station-quarter medians of the five variables used below.
3. Join SubsurfSAR of the *preceding* quarter; keep fluorescence same-quarter, not lagged.
4. Fit near-bed oxygen on lagged log(1+SubsurfSAR) with station, month and year fixed
   effects and covariates: fluorescence, near-bed temperature (solubility), near-bed
   salinity (stratification proxy), depth.
5. Refit with a substrate × effort interaction; repeat the whole fit on surface oxygen as
   the placebo.
6. Report every coefficient against the two nulls above, never a textbook p-value.

## What a result would, and would not, license

The obvious confound: trawlers fish where fish are, and fish are where productivity is, so
any raw effort–oxygen association is that association read backwards. Three things break it,
and only together — the within-station fixed effect removes everything time-invariant about
a place (depth, substrate, mean productivity, distance from a source); fluorescence absorbs
the time-varying part of the same confound; and the substrate interaction is a prediction
the confound does not make, since productivity-driven effort would depress oxygen wherever
trawlers work, while resuspension can only do it where reduced fines exist.

A mud-confined, bed-only coefficient outside both nulls would license exactly this:
*trawling effort in the preceding quarter is associated with lower near-bed oxygen at Danish
stations, 2016–2021, on fine substrate, beyond a null preserving the spatial and temporal
structure of effort.* Not a budget attribution; not a causal claim (effort is not randomised,
and quarterly SAR cannot separate the trawl pass from what followed it); nothing about fauna
outcomes O3/O7, since no fauna variable is here; nothing about the fleet VMS cannot see.

A null is weakly informative: six years, 8,396 observations, quarterly effort and a
modelled SAR missing the inshore fleet can all fail to see a real effect. And there is no
pre-trawling baseline — trawling predates the 1980 record start, so this is contrast across
places and quarters, never before-and-after.

**Overlap, precisely.** Effort is quarterly 2016–2021 against a monthly 1980–2026 record:
24 quarters, 8,396 near-bed station-months, 10.9% of `oxy_bed`. OSPAR's 2009–2020 layers
would raise that to 17,977 station-months at 325 stations, but at **annual** resolution,
destroying the lag structure that makes the within-station arm a test rather than a
correlation. The 0.05° cell is coarse against the station field — 262 of 791 cells hold more
than one station — so effort is a shared regressor and errors must cluster on the c-square.
