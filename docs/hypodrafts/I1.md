# I1 — Changing station network

**Hypothesis.** Stations were added, moved and dropped over the record, so a published
trend can be a trend in where you looked.

**Prior work this builds on.** Two non-exchangeable observing systems exist (293
summer-peaked, 481 year-round, 1.57 mg/l apart in Jun–Sep near-bed oxygen, d = 0.66) and
their mixture changes over time. That establishes the *ingredients* of a composition
artefact, not that any published number moved because of it. This draft tests that step.

## 1. The observable consequence, stated so it can fail

Hold the station set fixed and the season fixed, and recompute the Jun–Sep near-bed
oxygen trajectory 1985–2024 in five-year eras.

- **I1 is supported** if the era-to-era change measured on the fixed set differs from the
  change measured on the full pooled record by more than the fixed set's own sampling
  band — i.e. the pooled trajectory lies outside the band in at least one era, with the
  discrepancy exceeding 0.2 mg/l (twice the raw quantisation step, §3).
- **I1 fails** if the two trajectories track each other inside the band in every era —
  a real possible outcome, and the interesting one: 72% of observations turned over
  between 1985 and 2024 (§2) without moving the number, and the composition objection
  could be set aside for this variable.

## 2. The data, with counts verified here

`docs/data/areas/stations_series.{json,bin}` — 679,422 station-months, 1,415 stations,
564 months from 1980-01, 9 variables; `.bin` is 5,435,376 bytes, exactly the 8 bytes × Σn
the layout implies. Variable used: `oxy_bed`: **76,980 rows, all finite, 1,327 stations**.

Jun–Sep, 1985–2024: **30,460 station-months, 1,167 stations**. Per era (n / stations):
1985–89 2,589/443 · 1990–94 5,734/603 · 1995–99 4,963/563 · 2000–04 4,448/503 · 2005–09
3,635/379 · 2010–14 3,263/254 · 2015–19 2,815/210 · 2020–24 3,013/211. Station count
halves while observation count falls by less — turnover, not merely decline.

**Balanced panel:** stations with ≥1 Jun–Sep `oxy_bed` observation in *every* one of the
eight eras — **64 stations, 8,485 station-months**, per-era n from 637 to 1,156. Neither
empty nor degenerate. Its complement, 21,975 of 30,460 Jun–Sep observations (**72.1%**),
is what turns over.

**Error classes.**
- Class 3 (aggregation): each row is already a *monthly median per station per depth*.
  Within-month visit count is gone, so I3's effort confound cannot be controlled inside
  this file: a station visited once and one visited eight times weigh the same.
- Class 2 (quantisation): 62.1% of `oxy_bed` values sit exactly on a 0.1 mg/l grid and
  75.0% on 0.05 — the residual is the median of an even count. Underlying raw is 0.1 mg/l.
- Class 6 (absent dimension): no time-of-day column exists anywhere in the record. Diel
  oxygen swing aliases into whichever hour the boat went, per station, unrecoverably.

**The register does not supply what I1 says it supplies.** `data/raw/oda/stations.csv`
(latin-1, 798,574 rows, 17 columns, 6,288 distinct `ObservationsstedNr`) matches only
**34 of the 1,415 series stations**. Worse, `StartDato` equals `SlutDato` on **796,990
rows (99.8%)**: the field holds a single visit date, not a station lifespan — class 4,
schema conflation, a column named as a validity period carrying an event date.
**HYPOTHESES.md's "Now held: the ODA register carries them" is wrong.** Presence must be
derived from the observation record itself, as above. (Flagged, not edited here.)

## 3. The null, under the constraint actually imposed

The constraint is *fixed station set × fixed season window*, which shrinks n from 30,460
to 8,485 and clusters it into 64 groups. The relevant null is therefore **not** the
sampling distribution of a median over 8,485 independent draws, and no published standard
error applies. Compute it:

**Primary null.** H₀: the pooled trajectory is what the fixed network would have shown.
Cluster bootstrap over the 64 panel stations — resample 64 *with replacement*, carry each
station's entire era-by-era Jun–Sep observation set, recompute Δ_fixed(e) = med(e) −
med(1985–89). 5,000 replicates give a percentile band per era; reject H₀ where
Δ_pooled(e) falls outside the 95% band. The station is the resampling unit because
observations are not independent within it; resampling observations would understate the
band by roughly √(obs per station).

**Secondary null, for the season channel.** Month-stratified station-label permutation:
within each (era, calendar-month) cell, permute station labels among observations. Era n,
month composition and every visit schedule stay fixed; only the association between
*which* station and *what value* is destroyed. It will almost certainly reject — stations
differ in depth and level by construction — so it is a check on the season channel, not a
test of I1. Do not present rejection here as support.

**Multiplicity.** Pre-register the 2020–24 vs 1985–89 endpoint contrast as primary;
treat the six intermediate eras as descriptive, Holm-corrected.

## 4. Procedure

1. Read `stations_series.bin` at the `oxy_bed` offset (483,680); decode uint16 station
   index, uint16 month index, float32 value; year = 1980 + month//12, calendar month =
   month%12 + 1. ~1 s, <200 MB.
2. Filter Jun–Sep, 1985–2024. Assign eras.
3. Balanced panel = intersection of the eight per-era station sets (64 stations).
4. Compute Δ_pooled(e) and Δ_fixed(e).
5. Run the two nulls above. 5,000 × 8 medians over ≤8,485 rows — seconds.
6. Repeat for `oxysat_bed`, `sal_bed`, `temp_bed`. Salinity and oxygen saturation are
   computed (from conductivity+temperature, and from oxygen+temperature+salinity), so
   these are not independent replications: a consistent result is one result, not four.

**On the classification circularity.** The 293/481 summer/year-round split was made on
each station's *whole* record, so a station that switched regime is labelled by its
aggregate — contaminated by the very time-variation under test. This design avoids the
problem rather than fixing it: the balanced panel is defined by **presence**, observable
per era, and no behavioural label enters the primary test. Any secondary analysis wanting
the regime label must assign it **within era**, which is feasible: **395 stations have
≥12 observations (all months) in ≥2 eras**, 206 in ≥4, 33 in all 8. Report the switch
rate before using a pooled label again.

## 5. What a result would and would not license

**Would license.** Whether, at 64 positions watched continuously for forty years, the
Jun–Sep near-bed oxygen change is the change the pooled record reports — and if not, a
quantified size for the composition contribution.

**Would not license.**
- Any statement about a water body. The 64 stations are wherever they happen to be; they
  are not a designed sample of anything, and aggregating them into Vandområder would
  import the model assumption this repo refuses.
- A claim that the fixed-panel trajectory is the *true* trend. The panel is
  survivorship-selected: stations that survive forty years are plausibly main-programme
  deep-water stations, systematically unlike the ones that came and went. Holding the
  network fixed trades a composition bias for a selection bias; it does not remove bias.
  The honest reading of agreement is "turnover did not move this number", not "this is
  what the sea did".
- Anything about oxygen *causes*. Composition is an instrument question.
- Transfer to other variables, seasons, or the pre-1985 record (1980–84 holds only 763
  Jun–Sep station-months over 109 stations, which is why it is excluded).

**Nothing here has been run.** The counts in §2 are verified reads of the files; no
trajectory, band or p-value is reported.
