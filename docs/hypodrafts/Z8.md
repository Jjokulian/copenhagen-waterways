# Z8 — The attenuation budget is never partitioned

**Testable now**, on a subset, with one term left by subtraction — said, not hidden.

## Where Z8 overstates

*"Denmark measures broadband Kd, which cannot be partitioned even in principle"* is half
right. From the governing method document, extracted here rather than summarised —
**TA M06 *Lyssvækkelse* v3, Marint Fagdatacenter, in force 04.06.2015** (link below):

- One band, mandated: *"skal der måles fotosyntetisk aktivt lys i intervallet 400-700 nm
  også kaldet PAR."*
- §2.5.1 **names the spectral mechanism this project rediscovered from the data** and
  prescribes deletion as the cure: *"Lysets spektrale sammensætning … ændrer sig med
  vanddybden … en ikke-lineær hældning. I disse tilfælde kan det være nødvendigt at
  udelade lysmålinger fra den nederste del af vandsøjlen."* It also drops the profile's
  bottom when resuspension is suspected — deleting the mineral signal a partition wants,
  and creating the start-depth freedom `light.py` measured (6.31 m against 8.18 m).
- §2.5.2 collapses composition into a constant: the Secchi→Kd factor runs 1.5–3.6 in
  Danish waters, "så lav som 0,6" where scattering dominates and "op til 4,1" where
  dissolved matter does — then is set to **2,3**. That range **is** the partition.

Absent **by protocol**, not by physics — and the archive carries composition channels the
protocol does not use.

## The falsifiable consequence

Per **cast** — never a water body, which is why `val_kd.bin` and `val_secchi.bin`,
indexed by DKCOAST, are unusable here.

1. **Ordering.** Ranking casts by the chlorophyll proxy does not reproduce the ranking
   by Kd, and casts above a Kd threshold fall into at least two composition types
   (high-FDOM/low-turbidity, high-turbidity/low-FDOM) rather than one.
2. **The constant.** `Kd · z_Secchi` on the same station-day varies with FDOM and
   Turbiditet after station and month are removed. TA M06's own 0.6–4.1 range predicts
   this; the fixed 2.3 denies it.
3. **The artefact.** The apparent-Kd decline with depth (LIGHT.md: bottom/top ratio 0.879
   for surface starts, rising to 0.987 below 5 m) **weakens as FDOM rises**, because CDOM
   absorption at short wavelengths narrows the spread of K(λ) across the PAR band that
   produces the decline. Sign predicted before looking; the magnitude needs an assumed
   K(λ) and is not part of the test.

Each can fail: (1) would leave Z8's attribution complaint empty here, (2) would make the
collapsed constant harmless, (3) would refute LIGHT.md's spectral reading.

## Data, with counts computed here

**`lys.csv.gz`** — 2,370,340 rows; **62,848 distinct station|date casts** with a fitted
Kd, 62,845 with a fit coefficient, **62,136 with `ZMin`, the depth the regression started
from** — 40.6% shallower than 0.6 m, only 1,290 deeper than 5.1 m. 249 rows are
field-shifted and must be dropped by field count, not silently. 566 stations, **all also
CTD stations** (of 1,527). *Class 7* — Kd is a regression the operator fitted over a
window the operator chose; *class 3* — a profile collapsed to one slope. Guard against
circularity: require the fit coefficient, so Secchi-derived Kd cannot enter.

**`ctd.csv.gz`** — all 18 `Parameter` values counted (17 named, one blank; accented
names truncate in my scan). The four this needs: **Lysdæmpning 5,217,330** · Fluorescens
6,783,570 · **Turbiditet 1,076,915** · **FDOM 890,921**, with `Photometer m…` 576,179 and
`Photometer reference` 531,071 beside them. The rest are channels this project already
uses, plus five under 240,000 rows.

**The two composition channels do not cover the same record, and that decides the shape of
the test.** Turbiditet: 1994-01-10 → 2026-09-07, 252 stations, **12,700 station-days**, FTU
throughout. FDOM: **2021-04-19** → 2026-09-07, 211 stations, **10,550 station-days**, ppb
throughout. The in-situ CDOM term exists for five years; before 2021 only satellite
`adg443` carries it.

**`Lysdæmpning` is the finding**: the underwater and surface readings TA M06 requires
survive **per depth**, so Kd can be refitted over a *stated* window instead of the
operator's — on the same cast as the three proxies: composition and light in one
frame. Check its unit first: attenuation, or transmissometer beam-c, which is a
different quantity.

Error classes on the proxies: *class 1* — ppb and FTU are sensor units, not absorption
coefficients; *class 4* — FDOM is fluorescence, not a(λ)CDOM, and turbidity is scattering,
not non-algal absorption; *class 5* — `SondeNr` is 999 on 83.5% of rows, so the per-cast
calibration that would convert units is unrecoverable. *Class 6* over all of it: no
wavelength, no clock time.

## Procedure

1. Cast table from `lys.csv.gz` via `formats.num()`: station, date, Kd, fit, `ZMin`,
   `ZMax`; keep casts with a fit coefficient.
2. Stream `ctd.csv.gz` once, cast-contiguous (flush on key change, count reopened keys),
   emitting per station|date the depth-median Fluorescens, FDOM and Turbiditet **over
   `ZMin…ZMax`** — the layer the Kd was fitted over, not the whole cast.
3. Inner-join on station|date; report how the joined subset differs from all lys casts in
   Kd, station and month — that selection is the first result.
4. Apportion Kd's variance among the proxies within station × month, then fit (1), (2)
   against `maaledybde.csv.gz` Secchi on the same station-day, and (3) — each against the
   permutation null below, reported as a distribution rather than a p-value. Repeat with
   satellite `adg443`/`aph443`/`bbp443`, the only route to pre-2021 CDOM.

## The null, under the constraint imposed

Not a regression's nominal F-null. Casts are clustered by station, seasonal and
autocorrelated, and the composition channels share a station's mean level with Kd.
**Permute the composition triple across casts within station × calendar month**,
preserving each channel's marginals and the station/season structure, and rebuild the
apportionment 1,000 times; the observed unique variance must beat that distribution. Same
permutation for (2) and (3). Nothing quoted — the number is whatever the permutation
returns, reported even when it exceeds the observed statistic. Second constraint, already
shown here: `matchup.py` found the satellite-matched subset is **not** a random sample
(matched median Kd 0.270 against 0.330 in the same months), so the satellite arm's null is
built on that subset.

## The residual, named

Kd = K_water + K_phyto + K_CDOM + K_NAP. On the in-situ arm **none of the four is in
absorption units**, so this yields a variance apportionment, not a budget, and the residual
holds the start-depth artefact, the operator's window choice and time of day. The satellite
arm splits three ways at best, `adg443` conflating CDOM with detritus. A residual is
testable only by measuring a part P out of it and checking it shrinks by exactly P — which
needs absorption in units: an AC-S or spectroradiometer profile beside the PAR cast, done
twice in Danish waters (Gonçalves-Araujo & Markager 2020, around Fyn, 2017 and 2019) and
never in monitoring. Until then that shrinkage test is joint with a spectral model.

## What it licenses

**Would:** a statement about the *record* — whether Kd's cast-to-cast variance at named
stations in named months is carried mainly by the chlorophyll channel, against a
permutation null, and whether 2.3 is composition-free. **Would not:** a physical budget, a
causal attribution, any per-water-body or national number, or any trend — matchup.py
already got both signs.
