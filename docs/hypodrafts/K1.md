# K1 — Silicon depletion and the diatom-to-flagellate shift

**Draft, not a result. Nothing here has been run end to end.** Every count below marked
*verified* was computed in this session from the file or endpoint named.

K1 was filed as needing "dissolved silicate alongside N and P at the same stations and
dates, and species-level phytoplankton counts", with the counts believed unreachable.
Both halves turn out to be openly reachable. The chlorophyll asset that looked like the
answer turns out not to be one, and that is settled by computation, not by caveat.

## 1. The on-disk plankton files cannot carry this hypothesis

`data/raw/cmems/grid/plankton__inner__{1997..2025}.nc` — 29 files, product
`OCEANCOLOUR_BAL_BGC_L3_MY_009_133` (`cmems_obs-oc_bal_bgc-plankton_my_l3-multi-1km_P1D`),
7 variables (CHL, DIATO, DINO, MICRO, NANO, PICO, CYANOBLOOM mask), daily,
327×223 cells at 0.01098° lat × 0.01772° lon, 54.41–57.99 N / 9.26–13.19 E. 24 GB with
the transparency files. The registry entry `LOCAL-CMEMS-PFT` says 1997–2016 and six
variables; it is stale on both.

Sampled 261,652 valid cell-days from 2020 and 58k–257k from each of 1998, 2005, 2012,
2025:

- `PICO+NANO+MICRO − CHL` never exceeds **1.9e-6 mg m⁻³**; `DIATO+DINO − MICRO` never
  exceeds **9.5e-7**. The split is an exact partition of CHL, not an independent retrieval.
- **`DIATO/CHL` is a fixed function of `CHL` alone.** Grouping by CHL to three decimals,
  the largest within-group spread of the ratio over any year is **2.8e-4**. At CHL = 1.000
  the ratio is 0.365009 (1998), 0.364999 (2005), 0.365002 (2012), 0.365001 (2020),
  0.365000 (2025); at CHL = 0.5 it is 0.33935 in every year. Across 28 years, all seasons,
  all 73,000 cells.

So the diatom field is a monotone relabelling of total chlorophyll and carries **zero**
information about who the biomass is. K1's distinguishing prediction — *composition moves
while total biomass need not* — has measure zero in this product: it cannot be expressed,
let alone tested. **Error class 7, model-as-datum**, in its strongest form. The files
remain a legitimate CHL field; they are not a community observation.

**Independence.** Of the 62,270 EMODnet silicate values, **90.7% carry EDMO 729 =
Aarhus University, Bioscience, Roskilde** — the same NOVANA production path as ODA,
re-served under a European label. Genuinely foreign: German DOD 5.1%, SMHI 2.8%,
ICES 0.7%, SYKE + FMI 0.8%. EMODnet is an *access* route, not a second measurement.
The phytoplankton counts are the same programme again. **This test has one production
path on each axis, and they are the same programme** — it can score a mechanism, it
cannot cross-validate one.

**Quantisation, computed:** 61.3% of the silicate values are exact multiples of 1/28
(e.g. 14.035714 = 393/28) — integer µg L⁻¹ converted with M = 28, not 28.09. Class 2,
about 0.3% in the value.

**Units, computed:** the abundance eMoF is `number / cubic decimetre` on 686 and
`number / cubic metre` on 356 of 1,042 March-2011 records — a factor of 1,000. Units are
consistent *within* an event and mixed *across* events. Class 4; ignoring it moves shares
by three orders of magnitude.

## 3. Observable consequence, stated so it can be falsified

The denominator has to be built, not assumed: the dataset also contains bacteria
(7.1e8 cells L⁻¹ in one event) and ciliates, and records identified above class have a
null `class` field. Denominator = records resolving inside the autotrophic protist
groups; the unassignable fraction is reported, not distributed.

> At a **sampling position** (never a water body), the pre-bloom silicon pool sets the
> diatom fraction the following bloom can reach. Regress the **March–April diatom share
> of phytoplankton carbon** on the **preceding January–February median surface Si:DIN**
> at the same position, with position fixed effects. K1 predicts a positive slope **that
> survives conditioning on total chlorophyll** — the share must move where the biomass
> does not.

**Falsified if:** the slope is indistinguishable from the null below; or it vanishes once
total chlorophyll is conditioned on, which would say composition is only tracking biomass.

## 4. The null, computed here

Not the statistic's nominal null. From the 62,270 EMODnet samples I built the Jan–Feb
median surface Si:DIN per year, **40 years with ≥20 samples, 1975–2021**. Its lag-1
autocorrelation is **ρ₁ = 0.417**; trend +0.0002 yr⁻¹ (flat, and confounded — see below).

- nominal iid 5% threshold on |r|, n = 40: **0.312**
- circular year-shift null, all 39 non-identity shifts: 95th pct **0.333**, finest
  attainable p = 1/40 = **0.025**
- phase-randomised surrogates preserving the series' own spectrum, 20,000 draws:
  **95th pct |r| = 0.490**, 99th pct 0.603

**The persistence-aware threshold is 1.57× the nominal one.** A correlation of 0.35 —
"significant" against the textbook null — is the 60th percentile of this null. The
surrogate threshold is the one to use.

**A second null the design must absorb:** the panel is not fixed. Jan–Feb contributing
positions run 16 (1986) to 69 (2016), sample counts 27 to 408, and the EDMO-729 share
0.53 to 1.00. Any across-year statistic on pooled medians is partly a statistic about who
sampled. Hence position fixed effects and a balanced-panel restriction; a station-level
permutation is *not* conservative enough because neighbouring stations share water.

## 5. Procedure

1. Fetch OBIS occurrences with `mof=true`, paged by `after`. March 2011 is 2,420 records
   / 6.1 MB in 1 request; the full 45 years is ~280 MB of JSON and must be paged to disk
   by year, not held.
2. Group by `eventID`. **Keep only events where every occurrence carries an abundance**
   — 29 of 40 complete, 9 partial, 2 none in March 2011 (class 5, and dropping partial
   events is the honest choice; report the drop).
3. Normalise units to cells L⁻¹ per the record's own `measurementUnit`.
4. Join to PEG on (AphiaID, SizeClassNo) for carbon per counting unit: **33% of
   abundance records match strictly, 69% on AphiaID alone**. Unmatched are higher-rank
   identifications (`Bacillariales`, `Dinoflagellata`) and synonyms (`Skeletonema
   costatum`). Below ~80% matched carbon the share is not usable; a WoRMS synonym pass
   is required first.
5. **The currency is not neutral.** On 23 complete March-2011 events the diatom share by
   carbon (median 0.182) and by cell number (median 0.188) rank-correlate only
   **0.540**. State the individuation rule; report both.
6. Chemistry: prefer ODA Vandkemi, which joins by station identity. The open EMODnet
   route does not join well — of 40 March-2011 phytoplankton events, **3 have a surface
   silicate sample within 5 km and 2 days; 11 within 25 km and 7 days**; median nearest
   distance 74 km. That is the binding constraint on the open-data-only version.

## 6. Direction, which a correlation does not give

Silicate is supplied by weathering and consumed by diatoms, so a contemporaneous
correlation is compatible with either arrow, and with the trivial one: a diatom bloom
draws Si down, so *low Si with many diatoms* is the drawdown, not the limitation. Three
things would license direction, in increasing strength:

1. **Temporal ordering** — winter pool before the bloom, as specified above, with the
   cross-lagged comparison run both ways: if the reverse path (last spring's diatom share
   → this winter's Si) is as strong, the design has failed and says so.
2. **An exogenous shifter** — riverine Si flux is set by hydrology, not by marine diatoms.
   ODA *Vandløb / Stoftransport* carries `Silicium` monthly per station with `Vandføring`.
   Discharge also delivers N, P and stratification, so the exclusion restriction is not
   clean; it is a partial instrument and must be reported as one.
3. **The asymmetric policy shock** — N fell after 1987 while Si has no anthropogenic
   source. A step in Si:DIN driven by the N term alone is exogenous to diatoms in a way
   the ratio's Si term is not.

## 7. What it would and would not license

It would license a coefficient — diatom carbon share against pre-bloom Si:DIN, at
sampling-position level, against the surrogate null, for a stated denominator and
currency, at this point in the accumulation of measure-spaces. It would **not** license a
statement about total production, about oxygen, about any water body, or about whether
reducing nitrogen helps — K1's appeal is that reducing N raises Si:N, and this design
measures the association, not the intervention. Two measure-spaces here are one
programme; the score has no independence claim behind it yet.
