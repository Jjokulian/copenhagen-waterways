# K1 — Silicon depletion and the diatom-to-flagellate shift

**Draft, not a result. Nothing here has been run end to end.** Every count below was
computed in this session from the file or endpoint named. K1 needs "dissolved silicate
alongside N and P at the same stations and dates, and species-level phytoplankton
counts". Both halves are openly reachable; the chlorophyll asset that looked like the
answer is not one, and that is settled by computation.

## 1. The on-disk plankton files cannot carry this hypothesis

`data/raw/cmems/grid/plankton__inner__{1997..2025}.nc` — 29 files,
`OCEANCOLOUR_BAL_BGC_L3_MY_009_133`, 7 variables (CHL, DIATO, DINO, MICRO, NANO, PICO,
CYANOBLOOM mask), daily, 327×223 cells at 0.01098° × 0.01772°, 54.41–57.99 N /
9.26–13.19 E. The registry entry `LOCAL-CMEMS-PFT` says 1997–2016 and six variables; it
is stale on both. Sampling 261,652 valid cell-days from 2020 and 58k–257k each from
1998, 2005, 2012, 2025:

- `PICO+NANO+MICRO − CHL` never exceeds **1.9e-6 mg m⁻³**; `DIATO+DINO − MICRO` never
  exceeds **9.5e-7**. The split is an exact partition of CHL, not a separate retrieval.
- **`DIATO/CHL` is a fixed function of `CHL` alone.** Binning CHL to three decimals, the
  largest within-bin spread of the ratio in any year is **2.8e-4**. At CHL = 1.000 the
  ratio is 0.365009 (1998), 0.364999 (2005), 0.365002 (2012), 0.365001 (2020), 0.365000
  (2025); at CHL = 0.5 it is 0.33935 in every year — 28 years, all seasons, 73,000 cells.

The diatom field is a monotone relabelling of total chlorophyll and carries **zero**
information about who the biomass is. K1's distinguishing prediction — *composition moves
while total biomass need not* — has measure zero here: it cannot be expressed, let alone
tested. **Error class 7.** These files are a legitimate CHL field, not a community
observation.

## 3. Observable consequence, stated so it can be falsified

The denominator must be built, not assumed: the dataset also holds bacteria (7.1e8 cells
L⁻¹ in one event) and ciliates, and records identified above class have a null `class`.
Denominator = records resolving inside the autotrophic protist groups; the unassignable
fraction is reported, not distributed.

> At a **sampling position** (never a water body), the pre-bloom silicon pool sets the
> diatom fraction the following bloom can reach. Regress the **March–April diatom share
> of phytoplankton carbon** on the **preceding January–February median surface Si:DIN**
> at the same position, with position fixed effects. K1 predicts a positive slope **that
> survives conditioning on total chlorophyll**: the share must move where biomass does not.

**Falsified if:** the slope is indistinguishable from the null below, or it vanishes once
chlorophyll is conditioned on — which would say composition only tracks biomass.

## 4. The null, computed here

Not the statistic's nominal null. From the 62,270 EMODnet samples I built the Jan–Feb
median surface Si:DIN per year: **40 years with ≥20 samples, 1975–2021**, lag-1
autocorrelation **ρ₁ = 0.417**, trend +0.0002 yr⁻¹ (flat, and confounded — see below).

- nominal iid 5% threshold on |r|, n = 40: **0.312**
- circular year-shift null, all 39 non-identity shifts: 95th pct **0.333**; finest
  attainable p = 1/40 = **0.025**
- phase-randomised surrogates preserving the series' spectrum, 20,000 draws: **95th pct
  |r| = 0.490**, 99th pct 0.603

**The persistence-aware threshold is 1.57× the nominal one.** A correlation of 0.35 —
"significant" against the textbook null — sits at the 60th percentile of this one.

**A second null to absorb:** the panel is not fixed. Jan–Feb contributing positions run
16 (1986) to 69 (2016), samples 27 to 408, the EDMO-729 share 0.53 to 1.00, so any
across-year statistic on pooled medians is partly a statistic about who sampled. Hence
position fixed effects and a balanced panel; a station-level permutation is *not*
conservative enough, because neighbouring stations share water.

## 5. Procedure

1. Fetch OBIS occurrences with `mof=true`, paged by `after`: March 2011 is 2,420 records
   / 6.1 MB in one request; 45 years ~280 MB, paged to disk by year, not held.
2. Group by `eventID`; **keep only events where every occurrence carries an abundance**
   — 29 of 40 complete, 9 partial, 2 none in March 2011 (class 5; report the drop).
3. Normalise to cells L⁻¹ per the record's own `measurementUnit`.
4. Join to PEG on (AphiaID, SizeClassNo) for carbon per counting unit: **33% of abundance
   records match strictly, 69% on AphiaID alone**; the unmatched are higher-rank
   identifications (`Bacillariales`) and synonyms (`Skeletonema costatum`). Below ~80%
   matched carbon the share is unusable — a WoRMS synonym pass first.
5. **The currency is not neutral.** On 23 complete March-2011 events the diatom share by
   carbon (median 0.182) and by cell number (0.188) rank-correlate only **0.540**. State
   the individuation rule; report both.
6. Chemistry: prefer ODA Vandkemi, which joins by station identity. The open EMODnet route
   joins badly — of 40 March-2011 events, **3 have a surface silicate sample within 5 km
   and 2 days, 11 within 25 km and 7 days**, median nearest distance 74 km. That is the
   binding constraint on an open-only version.

## 6. Direction, which a correlation does not give

Si is supplied by weathering and consumed by diatoms, so a contemporaneous correlation
fits either arrow, and the trivial one: a bloom draws Si down, so *low Si with many
diatoms* is drawdown, not limitation. Three things would license direction:

1. **Temporal ordering** — the winter pool before the bloom, cross-lagged both ways: if
   the reverse path (last spring's share → this winter's Si) is as strong, the design has
   failed and says so.
2. **An exogenous shifter** — riverine Si flux is set by hydrology, not by marine diatoms;
   ODA *Vandløb / Stoftransport* carries `Silicium` monthly per station with `Vandføring`.
   Discharge also delivers N, P and stratification, so this is a partial instrument and
   must be reported as one.
3. **The asymmetric policy shock** — N fell after 1987 while Si has no anthropogenic
   source, so a step in Si:DIN driven by the N term alone is exogenous to diatoms in a
   way the Si term is not.

## 7. What it would and would not license

A coefficient — diatom carbon share against pre-bloom Si:DIN, at sampling-position level,
against the surrogate null, for a stated denominator and currency, at this point in the
accumulation of measure-spaces. **Not** a statement about total production, oxygen, any
water body, or whether cutting nitrogen helps: K1's appeal is that cutting N raises Si:N,
and this measures an association, not that intervention.
