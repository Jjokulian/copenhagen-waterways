# [C4 — Baltic inflow events](../hypodrafts/C4.md "Baltic inflow events")

*Numbers shown as quotations are carried from this page as committed at `c601feb`: nothing in the repository stores them yet, so each says what the page said, not that it was re-derived.*

## 1. The observable consequence

The falsifiable half of [C4](../hypodrafts/C4.md "Baltic inflow events") is **ordered propagation**: a wind-forced inflow event at month
τ produces a bottom-salinity anomaly whose **peak lag increases monotonically along the
path** Belts/Øresund → Arkona → Bornholm → Gotland Deep ([−200 m](../SOURCES.md#F-9f27d579a1)). Pre-registered windows,
from a bolus crossing [~350 km](../SOURCES.md#F-904c08ffd7) of bed at a few cm s⁻¹ with a stall in the Bornholm Basin:
strait bed [0–2 months](../SOURCES.md#F-877d8f0c8d), Arkona [0–3](../SOURCES.md#F-98aad14ea1), Bornholm [1–6](../SOURCES.md#F-c3413b83a9), Gotland salinity [3–15](../SOURCES.md#F-bbe60c2001), Gotland oxygen
[3–18](../SOURCES.md#F-f2cfdf21f2) decaying over [1–3 years](../SOURCES.md#F-ba4ae8b3b0). **The lag structure is asserted, not fitted**; the test is
whether the peaks fall in it, in order.

**Falsified if** the peak-lag vector is not monotone along the path, or the Gotland
salinity peak sits at lag ≤ [0](../SOURCES.md#F-7b446ec6c0), or the ordering statistic (Kendall τ of peak lag against
along-path distance) lies inside §3's null.

Secondary: an event should raise `sal_bed` **without** raising `sal_surf`. If both rise
together it is barotropic exchange with no stratification consequence, and [C4](../hypodrafts/C4.md "Baltic inflow events")'s
"strengthens stratification above them" clause fails at the Danish end.

## 3. The null, under the constraint I impose

I compare **one** strait composite against **one** Gotland series over 1995–2024. Under
that constraint the usual quoted null — "significant at n = [360 months](../SOURCES.md#F-aa3f82168f)" — is wrong by
an order of magnitude: Gotland deep salinity is a slow reservoir with effective sample size
of order ten. **Compute, do not quote.**

- **`N1`, primary: circular shift of the event index by whole years**, [31](../SOURCES.md#F-ed5f999f55) non-zero shifts
  over 1993–2024. Whole years preserve event seasonality, the summer sampling bias and
  every series' autocorrelation; only the alignment dies. **p floor [1/32 = 0.031](../SOURCES.md#F-a6180fe679) — state
  it, never exceed it.**
- **`N2`: [10,000](../SOURCES.md#F-be6402201d) Fourier phase-randomised surrogates** of each response, preserving spectrum
  and marginal. Continuous p, but it Gaussianises inflow's pulse-like character. `N1` and `N2`
  bound the answer from two sides; take the more conservative.
- **The [20 stations](../SOURCES.md#F-56c5c02e83) are not [20](../SOURCES.md#F-3a4ad97aa4) replicates** — they are [20](../SOURCES.md#F-dc305aa145) positions in one hydrographic
  regime. The statistic is the median across positions of a per-station z-anomaly, the
  panel shifts as one, and the null is over **time only**: cross-station spread buys
  precision, not degrees of freedom. Not a water-body aggregation — positions are listed
  individually under a stated depth-and-box rule, and the sensitivity run is [20](../SOURCES.md#F-9c81e78ae2)
  single-station fits reported as a *distribution of lags*, not [20](../SOURCES.md#F-aeeeb4e4b6) tests. If only the
  composite shows the signal, the composite made it.

## 4. Procedure

1. Build the wind index above; winter maxima; top *k* onsets (k = [3, 5, 8](../SOURCES.md#F-683f80ceff) pre-declared,
   not chosen after seeing the answer).
2. Per station, subtract that station's **own** month-of-year median from `sal_bed`,
   `sal_surf`, `oxy_bed`; divide by its IQR. Never pool before de-seasoning.
3. Same for `sob_ark`/`sob_bor` (monthly means) and Gotland `so`/`oxy` at [−200 m](../SOURCES.md#F-7d4fec57bd), after
   masking > [1](../SOURCES.md#F-a3d12f1832)e30, in native units.
4. Superposed-epoch composite at lags [−12](../SOURCES.md#F-4f36641054)…+[24 months](../SOURCES.md#F-ecbe0fdcf7) per site; record the peak lag.
5. Statistic: Kendall τ of peak lag against along-path distance (Belts [0 km](../SOURCES.md#F-8beaadc7f2), Arkona [~120](../SOURCES.md#F-25bf6bc834),
   Bornholm [~230](../SOURCES.md#F-0f1642bfa5), Gotland [~380](../SOURCES.md#F-d8fd7aaf64)).
6. Recompute τ under `N1` and `N2`.
7. Secondary: same on `sal_bed − sal_surf`; biphasic check on `oxy_bed`.

## 5. What a result would and would not license

**Would.** A clean falsification if the order breaks — cheap, and the statistic is robust
to every calibration error in §2. Otherwise weak corroboration, capped at p ≈ [0.03](../SOURCES.md#F-bc7be514a3).

**Would not.** (a) The effective event count is **[2–3](../SOURCES.md#F-fe5ed5ceb1)**, not [360 months](../SOURCES.md#F-2e80ab3be5); no effect size is
estimable, and the Gotland *oxygen* limb (2005-[11](../SOURCES.md#F-7b445bcb7e)→2024-[12](../SOURCES.md#F-0b92146dd6)) holds at most one large event —
n ≈ [1](../SOURCES.md#F-1ba5774023), no p at all, reportable only as narrative. (b) Nothing about [C4](../hypodrafts/C4.md "Baltic inflow events")'s contribution to
**[O1](../HYPOTHESES.md "Oxygen deficit")** in Danish water: the straits are the valve for a basin whose deep water Denmark does
not produce, so a ventilated Gotland Deep does not transfer to Danish coastal deficit, and
this design cannot make it. (c) It does not test "entirely exogenous to Danish policy" —
exogeneity of the *wind* is assumed, and exogeneity of the *consequences* is a different
claim. (d) Salinity is computed from conductivity and temperature, saturation from oxygen,
temperature and salinity: `sal_bed`, `temp_bed`, `oxysat_bed` are **one** piece of
evidence, not three.

**Smallest improvement:** fetch the IOW/Mohrholz MBI statistics (`DATA_QUEUE.md:49`),
replacing a self-built proxy with the published chronology [C4](../hypodrafts/C4.md "Baltic inflow events") names and turning the §2
coincidence at 2003-01-18 and 2014-12-20 from suggestive into checked.
